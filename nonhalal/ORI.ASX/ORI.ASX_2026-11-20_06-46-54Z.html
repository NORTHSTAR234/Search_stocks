Why the price moved

A. Why has Orica fallen despite higher operating earnings?

Underlying profits improved in FY2026, but an undefined acquisition, weaker cash conversion, an ex-dividend adjustment and a falling Australian market complicate the near-term valuation.

◇
A possible mining-chemicals acquisition raises financing questions.

Orica confirmed on 20 November at 08:26 AEDT that it had reached Stage 2 of the competitive sale process for Syensqo’s Technology Solutions business. There is no agreed purchase price or binding contract, so cash/debt/dilution implications cannot yet be measured; the same-day price fall makes this a strong plausible contributor, not established sole causation. Company announcement · 20 Nov · Capital Brief · 20 Nov CONFIRMED FACT + PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▣
Reported operating growth has not translated into cash.

FY2026 underlying EBIT grew 7% to A$1,056.9m and underlying NPAT grew 9% to A$589.7m, while operating cash flow fell to A$758.3m from A$949.2m, after A$344.2m of cash significant-item outflows. This gap can weigh on valuations and borrowing capacity, although the results were published 12 November rather than on the sale-announcement day. FY26 ASX filing · 12 Nov CONFIRMED FACT; PRICE CAUSATION NOT VERIFIED

◷
The ex-dividend date mechanically lowered price comparisons.

The stock went ex-dividend on 17 November for the declared A$0.355 final distribution, which is payable 17 December only to shareholders on the 18 November record. Raw prices may reflect losing that entitlement on 17 November; this does not explain the separate 20 November trading response by itself. Orica dividend table · Nov 2026 CONFIRMED FACT + LIMITED MECHANICAL INFERENCE

Wider market losses added another potential headwind.

Investing.com records a 1.58% fall in the S&P/ASX 200 on 20 November, versus the StockAnalysis-reported 5.19% drop in ORI over the same session. The source-matched indicative divergence is −3.61 percentage points; market correlation is not proof that macro selling caused Orica's company-specific move. A valuation-dependent purchase should depend on proof of cash conversion and deal funding—not merely a share-price fall. FY26 filing · 20 Nov acquisition update
