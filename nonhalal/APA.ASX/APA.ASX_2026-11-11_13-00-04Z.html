E. Growth opportunities

  1. Pipeline expansions and Sturt Plateau: new contracted connections can add EBITDA with inflation-linked escalation.
  2. Basslink regulated economics: conversion into a regulated asset should enhance cash-flow visibility (subject to execution and regulation).
  3. ~A$3.5bn FY27–FY29 organic growth pipeline: solar/BESS, grid reliability and gas firming support energy transition infrastructure demand. Growth capital must clear financing and cost-of-capital hurdles to add equity value.
