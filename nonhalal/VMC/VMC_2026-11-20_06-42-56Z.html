Why the price moved

A. Why did VMC fall with the wider market despite pricing strength?

Higher selling prices support the business, but lower construction volumes and fuel costs have kept earnings under pressure; Thursday's decline also occurred alongside a broad market sell-off.

▥
Construction volumes are weak despite pricing gains.

Vulcan's 29 October third-quarter filing shows revenue down about 5% to $2.185 billion and aggregates shipments down 3% year over year. Lower demand can offset price rises and leave profit growth disappointing. These quarterly facts explain the investment concern but do not prove they caused the November 19 daily decline. SEC filing · Oct 29, 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT / POSSIBLE PERSISTENT PRESSURE

◈
Diesel inflation is eroding part of the operating gain.

The third-quarter report and call say aggregate cash unit costs rose 7% per ton including diesel, versus around 3% excluding fuel. The cost difference helps explain why price improvements do not automatically lift profit. Energy prices may remain volatile, so this is a plausible longer-term valuation pressure rather than established same-day causation. Vulcan Q3 · Oct 29, 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

↗
The company still expects improving quarry economics.

Vulcan targets approximately $2.4 billion in 2026 adjusted EBITDA and described mid-to-high-single-digit aggregate pricing growth potential in 2027. This is management's outlook, not guaranteed earnings. The outlook offers counterevidence to an exclusively negative interpretation of the stock. Vulcan outlook · Oct 29, 2026 · CONFIRMED COMPANY GUIDANCE

D. Company operations, financial health and cash flow

Vulcan is a leading US supplier of construction aggregates, with asphalt and concrete exposure. In Q3 ended Sep 30 2026, sales were $2,184.6m, down ~4.7% from $2,291.5m; attributable net income $362.3m versus $374.9m (−3.4%); diluted EPS $2.80 versus $2.82. Aggregates shipments decreased 3%; freight-adjusted prices increased roughly 3.8%, but fuel-influenced unit cash costs rose around 7%. Company-wide adjusted EBITDA margin ~32.3%, modestly above 32.1% year earlier. SEC Q3 10-Q; Earnings release · Oct 29. Nine-month operating cash inflow $971.0m, with reported investment in existing operations roughly $548m (includes PP&E accrual changes): this is not a strictly comparable cash CAPEX figure, so current-period exact free cash flow is NOT VERIFIED. Sep 30 cash ~$172.4m, debt ~$4,680.6m, gross debt / trailing adjusted EBITDA ~2.0× (net ~1.9×), manageable but meaningful cyclical leverage. Financial position. Capital investment and acquisitions can constrain cash available for distributions even when accounting earnings rise.
