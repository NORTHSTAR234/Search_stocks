Why the price moved

A. Why did Samsung fall despite record profits?

Revenue and earnings are strong, yet investors care about whether those profits can persist in the face of chip export rules, supply cycles and enormous new production spending. The precise causal breakdown of the share-price change has not been established.

⌁
Chip-export restrictions have put the sector on alert.

On 17 November 2026, Reuters reported Samsung and TSMC shares fell following new US curbs on China's access to advanced AI chips. The restrictions can affect accessible markets and chip-equipment expectations. This is a confirmed news event and plausible price contributor, not proof it caused all of Samsung’s weekly decline. Reuters · 17 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED EVENT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▣
Record memory earnings have raised the bar.

Samsung disclosed third-quarter revenue of ₩195.3 trillion and operating profit of ₩107.4 trillion on 29 October. Investors can still worry that memory prices and extraordinary profit margins will eventually normalise. That valuation concern is an inference, not a confirmed explanation for an individual day’s price move. Samsung · 29 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED RESULTS / INFERRED VALUATION CONCERN

▤
The next expansion cycle requires heavy capital.

The board disclosed roughly ₩84.4 trillion of 2026 capital expenditure, including ₩76.8 trillion for semiconductor operations. New capacity may support AI-memory growth but can constrain future free cash flow if demand slows. No verified source quantifies what part of the recent share decline arose from the spending plan. Samsung IR · 29 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED PLAN / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

↘
The stock has lagged a softer Korean market.

Samsung closed at ₩206,000 on 13 November and ₩196,900 on 19 November, down -4.42%. Over exactly those sessions, the KOSPI moved from 5,290.53 to 5,137.89, down -2.89%, meaning Samsung underperformed by -1.53 percentage points. This measured divergence alone does not tell us why investors sold. Investing · 19 Nov 2026 · Yahoo KOSPI · 19 Nov · CONFIRMED OBSERVATIONS / NO CAUSAL CLAIM

E. Growth opportunities

AI-server investment benefits high-bandwidth memory, modern DRAM and other performance-oriented semiconductor products. Samsung's Oct 29 issuer results describe premium-memory expansion and foundry plans involving new mobile/4nm processing products. A competitive HBM4 ramp could raise both unit volumes and prices; chip qualification, yields and customers still matter more than a broad AI narrative. Samsung Q3 and Q4 outlook

The long-lived ₩84.4T expansion plan can create future capacity if demand survives, but also increases depreciation and execution exposure. Foundry gains and diversified mobile/display revenues offer upside beyond memory while introducing their own lower-margin and consumer-cycle constraints. Reassess after confirmed order/qualification news and 2027 operating margin evidence.
