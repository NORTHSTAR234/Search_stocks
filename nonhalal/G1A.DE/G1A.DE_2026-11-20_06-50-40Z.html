Why the price moved

A. Why did GEA shares fall despite faster orders?

Orders rose sharply, but investors still have to price delivery timing, convertible financing and whether growth yields distributable earnings.

◇

Convertible issuance adds uncertainty about future shares.

On 17 November GEA placed €300m of 2031 convertible bonds, with settlement expected around 24 November. The 17 November close of €69.55 was 1.35% below the previous €70.50. Potential conversion into new or existing shares may affect expectations, but the timing does not prove causation. GEA announcement · 17 Nov 2026 · FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▤

Orders are increasing faster than realised revenue.

The issuer reported €1,650.7m of Q3 2026 order intake (+20.3%) and €1,461.2m of revenue (+7.0%). Large projects still need time to become sales and cash; stronger orders alone are not a share-price guarantee. GEA Q3 release · 9 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT / INTERPRETATION

◎

Margins improve while working capital still matters.

GEA earned €260.9m of Q3 adjusted EBITDA (+12.6%), with a 17.9% margin, and Q3 free cash flow improved to €134.7m from €52.2m. Restructuring expense and increased working capital mean profit quality must be monitored rather than assumed permanent. GEA Q3 statement · 9 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT / CAUSALITY UNVERIFIED

D. Moat, competitive position and valuation

GEA's established industrial customer base and installed processing equipment support services and parts demand. Q3 service share of revenue was 40.9%; quarter order book was ~€3.72bn at 30 Sep 2026. Large orders (€294.8m in Q3 versus €64.4m a year earlier) can create lumpiness as well as growth; the risk is delivery, procurement and project margins. Issuer Q3 release.

Valuation: nine-month EPS of €2.15 mechanically annualises to roughly €2.87 at a 4/3 factor, so €69.55 / €2.87 = ~24.3×. This rough partial-year run-rate is not an actual FY2026 EPS, consensus forward P/E or fair value; it ignores seasonality, special costs and Q4 delivery. Relevant operational peer comparisons include Krones and Alfa Laval but a reliable dated aligned EV/EBITDA or forward-P/E peer matrix was not reproduced. Thus numerical valuation-based buy zones remain NOT RATED. Issuer statement.
