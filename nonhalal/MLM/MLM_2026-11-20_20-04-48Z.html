Why the price moved

A. Why did MLM fall even as reported sales grew?

The third-quarter headline sales were strong, but GAAP per-share earnings fell and the enlarged acquisition-funded balance sheet leaves investors sensitive to borrowing costs. These facts are context, not proof they caused the 19 November price move.
