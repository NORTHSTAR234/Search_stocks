E. Clinical research, growth catalysts and specific risks

Research promise: Multi-antigen recognizing (MAR) T-cell design may offer a way to target cancer cells without genetic engineering. Early Phase 1 company-reported responses are encouraging; the award provides external research support. Risks: only 12 evaluable participants in the reported efficacy subgroup, uncontrolled early-stage trial, uncertain response durability, manufacturing reproducibility, payer economics, regulatory hurdles, binary data events, potential share dilution, widening microcap spreads and restricted-use grants. An award is not a regulatory endorsement of efficacy.

Peer context: An approved and commercially scaled cell-therapy company is not an appropriate EPS/DCF peer for a clinical-stage single-platform microcap. Robust clinical and financing milestones, not current EPS, are the decisive comparators.
