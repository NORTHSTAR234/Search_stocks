A · Price movement and investment thesis

Why the price moved

A. Why have SK hynix shares fallen despite record profits?

Sales and operating profit are exceptionally strong, yet the market has been marking down the price it will pay for their durability. Exact attribution of the recent fall to one announcement has not been established.

▦
Huge quarterly earnings have not prevented profit-cycle concerns.

On 28 October SK hynix reported preliminary Q3 revenue of ₩104.6155tn and operating profit of ₩83.3338tn, an 80% operating margin. These figures demonstrate strong AI-memory pricing, but the market can discount future margin normalization even while today's profits rise. Issuer · 28 Oct 2026 CONFIRMED RESULTS; future concern INFERENCE

↘
Broader Korean equities also retreated in this window.

Between the completed KRX sessions on 30 October and 19 November, SK hynix fell 16.57% versus 10.35% for KOSPI. The extra 6.23-point underperformance indicates stock-specific weakness but does not prove whether foreign flows, valuations or an issuer development caused it. Stock close history · KOSPI close history CONFIRMED PRICES; CAUSE UNVERIFIED

◫
Large nonoperating swings complicate the profit picture.

Q3 net income of ₩39.7756tn was much lower than operating profit of ₩83.3338tn, so relying on the operating headline alone misses important investment/financing/accounting effects. The precise forward recurrence of this gap cannot be established from a preliminary quarter, and valuing today's EPS as permanent is unsafe. D. Is the apparent bargain durable?

Trailing P/E near 4.5× and market-reported forward P/E near 2.3× make the shares look inexpensive in an ordinary earnings-multiple screen. For memory producers, however, a low multiple near a margin peak may mean the market expects earnings to decline sharply; comparing a forward estimate to Samsung's trailing ~6.2× is only a rough context, not a like-for-like discount. No defensible cycle-normalized EPS, peer-adjusted discount rate and stable FCF model were verified, so intrinsic fair value is NOT RATED. E · Product, moat, cash conversion

E. Growth catalysts and cash allocation

The central growth variable is whether advanced HBM4/HBM3E contracts remain profitable as capacity ramps and packaging competition rises. Management reported that more than half of 2027–28 capacity was covered by long-term agreements; actual shipping mix, pricing, capex and free cash flow must validate those commitments. Quarter-end cash around ₩114.5tn against ₩15.8tn borrowing is a substantial buffer, yet a nominally enormous shareholder-return plan should be tested against subsequent FCF and share-cancellation filings, not assumed completed. Issuer 28 Oct 2026 release.

Company-announced Q3 dividend ₩20,000/share comprises ₩10,000 ordinary plus ₩10,000 special. At ₩1,183,000 that is ~1.69% for this single declared payout; it is not a verified recurring annual dividend yield.
