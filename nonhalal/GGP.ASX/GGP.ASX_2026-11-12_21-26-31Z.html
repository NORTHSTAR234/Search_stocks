E. Growth opportunities

  • Telfer underground and mine-life extensions: West Dome drilling, open-pit cutbacks and vertical stockwork studies could convert new resources into additional future ore, but are not yet proven reserve additions.
  • Havieron development: Board-approved investment decision with first gold expected in FY29; significant longer-run high-grade gold/copper opportunity using Telfer infrastructure, beyond the 12-month earnings horizon.
  • High gold prices and copper by-product: Exposure to stronger realised metals prices and potential operating leverage. Gold put options on ~150koz near A$4,850/oz can protect downside while preserving upside as detailed by management, though not risk-free.
