E. Growth opportunities

Fortune Electric supplies power transformers, switchgear and grid equipment. Its own sustainability materials say the Guanyin 3B plant began operation in Q1 2026 and plans a Taichung ultra-high-voltage transformer plant for Q2 2027. The manufacturer targets power-grid reinforcement and larger North American export transformer demand; however commissioning, fulfilment, working capital, export FX and order acceptance determine whether headline demand produces future profits. Issuer sustainability report.

Reasons new orders can help: more capacity, higher-voltage product mix and grid renewal demand. Reasons execution may lag: long manufacturing cycles, production commissioning and inconsistent revenue recognition. Do not count order deposits or management capacity plans as earned sales.
