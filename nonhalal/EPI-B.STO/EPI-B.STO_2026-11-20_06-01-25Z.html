Why the price moved

A. Why did Epiroc shares weaken despite higher orders?

The shares underperformed the Swedish blue-chip index during 13–19 November, despite rising mining orders; earnings conversion, analyst expectations and the market sell-off may all matter, but none alone proves the cause.

▤
More equipment orders have not turned into stronger cash flow.

Epiroc reported Q3 organic order growth of 12% and revenue growth of 7%. Operating cash flow declined from SEK 2,476m to SEK 1,438m, partly amid inventory needs; this can reduce the cash investors anticipate from new equipment orders. A plausible valuation pressure, not a proven cause of November trades. Epiroc Q3 · 28 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED figures / PLAUSIBLE market explanation

⌁
Lower analyst price targets may reflect elevated expectations.

A dated 19 October brokerage update downgraded Epiroc from overweight to equal-weight, reducing the target from SEK 295 to 270. Reported analyst revisions show valuation debate, but do not establish a direct trigger for each November trading session. MarketScreener analyst news · 19 Oct 2026 · REPORTED ANALYST VIEW

Aramine acquisition expands the aftermarket services opportunity.

On 16 November, Epiroc announced completion of Aramine, a mining spare-parts provider. The expansion may support the higher recurring-service mix; nevertheless, the shares remained volatile and the price reaction cannot be attributed solely to this announcement.
