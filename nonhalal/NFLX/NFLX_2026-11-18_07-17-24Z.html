E. Competition and business quality

Netflix’s global streaming distribution, diversified content library, original programming and ad-supported tier are business strengths. Rival streaming video, YouTube, pricing sensitivity, content spending, rights costs and variable hit production all challenge engagement and margins. Reported member retention is healthy; viewing-hours acceleration has not been demonstrated in this research. The core longer-term thesis depends on turning viewer attention into durable subscriber, advertising and cash-flow growth. Netflix strategy and operating narrative.
