What was actually verified

AAOIFI official Shari'ah Standard No. 21 remains listed on the official portal as of November 2026. Numerators, source reporting periods, tradability proxies, valuation caveats, purification calculations where available and specific unresolved conditions are preserved in each ticker's linked HTML file. The files are not a uniform new independent real-time filing review: many individual reports were already screened on 12–13 November 2026 and retain those financial evidence limitations. This batch verified file existence, audited/report statuses, corrected ethical classification and documented individual screens available at the time.

AAOIFI "share tradability" concerns assets, eligible benefits and rights, potentially subject to market valuation; a book proxy above 30% is not automatically a confirmed tradability pass. For non-equity instruments, including APA stapled units, ordinary-share SS21 can be insufficient. Missing purification amounts must not be replaced by dividend-based guesses.
