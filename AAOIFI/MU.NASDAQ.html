A. Business activities and objectives

Micron designs and manufactures memory/storage semiconductors, including DRAM, NAND, NOR, high-bandwidth memory and related products. The principal business is not inherently prohibited. The SEC filing also discloses conventional fixed-income securities, debt notes and foreign exchange/commodity hedges whose Shariah treatment must be considered as ancillary activity. The existence of hedges or notional contracts does not by itself prove unlawful realized income.
