A. Business activity

Micron's core operation is design, manufacturing and sale of DRAM/HBM memory and NAND/flash storage chips used in AI/cloud systems, data centres, mobile, clients and vehicles. Its business-unit disclosures cover Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile and Client, and Automotive and Embedded; no intrinsically prohibited core segment is identified.

The publicly available restated certificate of incorporation says its objective is any lawful corporate activity under Delaware law and does not expressly name interest-based business or prohibited products as an objective. A general lawful-activity clause is not a separate Shariah certification. Micron has ancillary conventional borrowing, deposit and bond investment positions, evaluated below.

Business-activity assessment: PASS. Sources: issuer Q4/FY26 results and issuer certificate of incorporation.

Identified minimum purification: (US$1,084,000,000 ÷ 1,131,000,000 shares) = US$0.95844 per period-end share. If the investor owned N shares on September 3, a calculation using identified interest only is US$0.95844 × N in USD.

  • Medium confidence: mixed-asset value is based on conservative booked tangible assets and inventory, not appraised realizable market value.
  • Standard freshness: AAOIFI's present online standards list still carries SS21, and no replacement ordinary equity rule was verified as of this screening. The publicly readable SS21 version checked is an older AAOIFI publication; later interpretative Shariah-board guidance may affect implementation.
