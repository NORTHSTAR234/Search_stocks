A. Business activity

Mining gold and copper is not intrinsically prohibited as a primary business activity. The group reports one mining/development/exploration operating segment in its FY2026 annual report. It recorded A$2,259.372m sales revenue primarily from gold and copper. No substantial prohibited operating segment was identified.

Ancillary financial dealings require classification: surplus cash held in interest-bearing accounts and term deposits; gold put options used to hedge production; A$24.984m of strategic ASX-listed equity securities; and a A$475m undrawn revolving conventional bank facility. A review of the complete memorandum/articles for the precise corporate-objects condition in SS21 §3/4/1 was not completed.
