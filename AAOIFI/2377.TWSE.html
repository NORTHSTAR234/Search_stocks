C. Accounting bridge

  • Conventional borrowing numerator: short-term borrowings NT$0; long-term bank borrowings NT$6.319107bn gross, consisting of NT$5.2bn unsecured at 1.55%–1.75% and NT$1.119107bn secured at 3M SOFR + 0.95%. The NT$25.378m current portion is included in the gross total, not double counted.
  • Interest-taking deposit numerator: time deposits within cash equivalents NT$4.418787bn; time deposits maturing three months to one year NT$9.4bn; pledged bank deposits NT$613.852m. The filing separately shows checking/demand deposits of NT$12.004705bn; because it does not identify which demand balances are interest-bearing, the base ratio excludes them and a conservative sensitivity includes them.
  • Non-interest cash: cash on hand/revolving funds NT$5.144m is excluded from the deposit numerator.
  • Interest-bearing securities/investments: other financial assets are shown separately and are not silently added to the AAOIFI deposit numerator. Financial assets at fair value through profit or loss included listed shares and FX derivatives; they did not produce a positive net prohibited-income amount in H1 2026.
  • Prohibited income: confirmed interest income NT$317.267m, comprising NT$268.569m from bank deposits and NT$48.698m from financial assets at amortised cost.
  • Opaque income sensitivity: if all NT$216.541m labelled “Others” in other income plus NT$5.635m “other revenues” in other gains/losses were conservatively treated as prohibited, the ratio would still be only about 0.51% of operating revenue.

D. Market-capitalisation convention

AAOIFI implementation convention — reporting-date market cap. The screen uses the 30 June 2026 TWSE closing price of NT$146 and approximately 844.8562m issued shares implied by paid-in capital of NT$8.448562bn at NT$10 par value. This gives market capitalisation of approximately NT$123.349bn. AAOIFI SS21 states market capitalisation for the borrowing/deposit tests but does not prescribe a 24- or 36-month averaging window.

As a sensitivity check, using the 11 November 2026 TWSE close of NT$145 gives market cap of about NT$122.504bn; the borrowing ratio becomes 5.16%, the clearly identified deposit ratio 11.78%, and the conservative deposit sensitivity 21.58%. The verdict does not change.

E. Tradability / asset composition

AAOIFI SS21 requires tangible assets, benefits and rights to represent at least 30% of total asset value where the mixed-asset tradability rule is relevant. At 30 June 2026, inventories alone were NT$58.565bn, or about 45.5% of total assets. Adding property, plant and equipment, right-of-use assets and investment property produces a conservative book-value proxy of about NT$68.962bn, or 53.6% of total assets. This comfortably exceeds 30%.

Limitation: SS21 refers to market value/value of tangible assets, benefits and rights; public interim statements provide book values. Because inventories alone exceed 30% by a wide margin, the book-value proxy has a substantial buffer, but it remains an implementation limitation.

F. Purification

For the six months ended 30 June 2026, confirmed prohibited income is NT$317.267m of interest income. Using approximately 844.8562m shares, confirmed prohibited income per share is:

NT$317.267m ÷ 844.8562m shares = NT$0.3755 per share

Confirmed minimum purification: NT$0.3755 per share. A deliberately conservative upper sensitivity that treats the opaque “Others” and “other revenues” lines as prohibited would be about NT$0.6385 per share, but those items are not established as prohibited and therefore are not included in the confirmed AAOIFI amount.

Under SS21, purification is linked to ownership at the end of the relevant financial period and is not dependent on a dividend being distributed. No user holding quantity was supplied, so investor purification is shares held at 30 Jun 2026 × NT$0.3755 for the confirmed minimum amount.

G. Data quality and unresolved issues

  • The latest verified issuer financial report exposed by MSI's official financial-report page at screening time is Q2 2026. A Q3 2026 English report was not verified, so the ratios use 30 June 2026, consistent with SS21's “last budget or verified financial position” requirement.
  • Demand deposits are not split between interest-bearing and non-interest-bearing balances. A conservative inclusion of all demand deposits still passes the 30% threshold comfortably.
  • Some “other” income descriptions are not decomposed. Treating the disclosed opaque items as prohibited still leaves the prohibited-income ratio far below 5%.
  • The tradability test uses a conservative book-value proxy because interim public data do not provide market values for all tangible assets, benefits and rights.

H. AAOIFI guidance

On the verified 30 June 2026 financial position, 2377.TWSE passes the AAOIFI SS21 business, borrowing, deposit, prohibited-income and tradability tests. This is a rules-based AAOIFI implementation assessment, not an AAOIFI certification or fatwa. The company still earns interest and uses conventional interest-bearing borrowings, so AAOIFI's exemption conditions and purification obligation remain relevant even though the ratios are within the permitted ceilings.
