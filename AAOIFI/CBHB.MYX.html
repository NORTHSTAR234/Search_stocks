E. Tradability / asset composition

Under the separate SS21 share-trading provision, tangible assets, benefits and rights should contribute at least 30% of total assets on the relevant valuation basis. The 2025 balance sheet records RM5.250m in property/plant/equipment and RM62.957m in contract assets; together their book values are only 23.62% of RM288.773m total assets. Crucially, recorded contract assets are principally conditional rights to consideration for services already delivered; whether and at what value those and other engineering contract benefits count for SS21 tradability is a Shariah-interpretation and market-value question. A book-value calculation alone cannot conclusively label the instrument non-tradable or tradable.

RESULT: SHARIAH REVIEW REQUIRED. Obtain a full valuation of eligible tangible assets, non-debt rights and benefits, and the relevant qualified-board application of SS21 before changing the classification. 2026 assets may have changed materially.

SS21 3/19 rights valuation detail (FY2025 book proxy; new 13 Nov review):

Physical PPE RM5,249,733 / total assets RM288,773,284 = 1.82%. If the entire RM62,956,748 of reported contract assets were eligible rights (not established), book proxy rises to only 23.62%. Contract assets primarily represent consideration for work performed but not yet billed and may be debt-like conditional receivables, not automatically tradable non-debt rights.

With the reported asset denominator unchanged, numerator needs RM86,631,985 to reach 30%, a shortfall of RM18,425,504 above PPE+entire contract-asset book proxy. If the contract-right carrying asset C is instead wholly eligible and replaced with its own independent market value V, with other assets assumed unchanged, Vmin = [0.30×(RM288,773,284−RM62,956,748)−RM5,249,733]/0.70 = RM89,278,897 (~41.8% above contract-asset book). The denominator then becomes T−C+V. This is a mathematical break-even, NOT evidence of legal eligibility or an appraised market price. A 10% or 20% haircut would reduce the below-threshold ratio further. Goodwill/cash/trade receivables are not quietly counted as qualifying assets. A Shariah-board contract classification plus independent rights valuation is required to decide this test.
