G. Data quality and unresolved issues

  • The primary source is the issuer's CPA-reviewed Q3 2026 consolidated report (published around 28–29 October 2026). It is the latest confirmed financial position for this screening; the audited annual report is a background cross-check.
  • IBKR supplied USD-converted Q3 summary and LSEG investment/financial panels, not the primary regulatory source. Q3 annual-style and TTM dollar numbers must not be mixed with nine-month TWD numerators without a consistent timeframe and currency.
  • The gross prohibited-income ratio requires classification of the NT$334.022m “other gains” component, FVTPL underlying instruments, and any gross/net offsets. The visible 0.450% calculation is diagnostic, not proof of a full maximum.
  • Interest-taking deposit breakdown lacks contract-level characterization. The extreme all-assets ceiling nevertheless robustly passes the financial 30% deposit limit at either relevant price.
  • Corporate constitutional objective clauses have not been independently subjected to a formal SS21 review.
  • Recent share-price declines alter percentage denominators but are not evidence that a manufacturing activity became prohibited.
