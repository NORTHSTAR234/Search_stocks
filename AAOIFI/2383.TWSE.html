A. Business activity

Elite Material Co., Ltd. (台光電子材料股份有限公司) makes copper-clad laminates, prepregs and multilayer electronic materials for printed circuit boards and applications including communications, servers, AI infrastructure and vehicles. Manufacturing these products is not an intrinsically prohibited primary business under the SS21 equity-screen premise. The stock is an ordinary Taiwan-listed equity, not a bond or derivative.

The consolidated filing discloses conventional bank debt, interest income and ancillary financial transactions. These are analyzed separately from operating activity. A complete certified copy of the corporate constitutional objectives has not been obtained; no claim that the corporate objects were independently Shariah-reviewed is made.
