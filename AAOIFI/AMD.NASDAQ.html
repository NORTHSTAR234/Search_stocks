Executive summary

AMD's semiconductor and computing business is permissible, its interest-bearing borrowing and conservative deposit ratios are far below AAOIFI's 30% limits, and even a deliberately harsh assumption that all reported long-term investment gains are prohibited leaves prohibited income below 5%. The separate tradability test is supported by a book-value proxy but carries lower confidence.

A. Business activity

AMD's primary activities are CPUs, GPUs, AI accelerators, data-centre products, FPGAs, embedded devices and related semiconductor technology. The primary business is permissible under the AAOIFI share-screening framework.

C. Accounting bridge

Conventional senior notes are included in the borrowing numerator. Cash and short-term investments are not silently treated as interest-taking deposits; instead the whole amount is used as a conservative upper-bound sensitivity check, which still remains far below 30%.

D. Market-capitalisation convention

AAOIFI SS21 specifies market capitalisation for the borrowing and deposit tests but does not specify a 24- or 36-month averaging window. Stock_screener therefore uses market capitalisation at or close to the financial-position date as an explicitly disclosed implementation convention and tests sensitivity where material.

E. Tradability / asset composition

Using reported inventory, property/equipment and identifiable technology/customer/trademark rights as a book-value proxy gives roughly 32.2% of total assets, above 30%. Because SS21 refers to value/market value rather than GAAP carrying value, this test is Medium confidence.

F. Purification

Confirmed interest income gives a minimum identifiable purification of about US$0.205 per share for the nine-month period. If all US$616M of reported long-term investment gains were also prohibited, a conservative upper bound is about US$0.581 per share.

G. Data quality and unresolved issues

The exact Shariah classification of individual long-term investments affects the purification amount but does not change the compliance verdict because even the extreme stress case stays below 5%.

H. AAOIFI guidance

AAOIFI-COMPLIANT. AMD passes the verified business, borrowing, deposit and prohibited-income tests with substantial headroom. The tradability calculation uses a disclosed book-value proxy and therefore carries Medium rather than High confidence.
