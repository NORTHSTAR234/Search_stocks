A. Business activity and corporate purpose

AMD sells CPUs, EPYC server processors, Instinct GPUs/AI accelerators, embedded devices, FPGAs and related software across Data Center, Client & Gaming and Embedded. These core semiconductor activities are not inherently impermissible under AAOIFI SS21. The amended corporate charter objective of interest dealing was not identified in the reviewed filing; this is not a legal opinion on every contractual purpose.

Relevant ancillary exposure: conventional debt securities and interest-bearing notes, publicly traded equity and warrant holdings, FX derivative hedges, customer warrant incentives, and commercial financing arrangements. Contracts should be reviewed by a scholar separately from determining the principally permissible semiconductor business. AMD also offers defense-grade computing components for radar, ISR and electronic warfare; end users and related ethical/AAOIFI contract implications cannot be inferred merely from product descriptions.

E. Share tradability and final AAOIFI finding

The separate SS21 asset-composition test is not interchangeable with debt/cash screens. At book values, inventory $11.463B + PPE $3.929B + acquired intangible assets/rights $15.101B = $30.493B, divided by total assets $94.812B, equals 32.162%. SS21 speaks of the relevant market value of tangible assets, benefits and rights. The GAAP carrying value of acquired rights has not been independently verified as that market value, and its classification requires Shariah review.

If rights are omitted pending proof, inventory + PP&E is $15.392B / $94.812B = 16.234%, below 30%. The book proxy exceeds the 30% threshold by just $2.049B; a 13.6% write-down would exhaust the cushion ONLY if the original $94.812B denominator were artificially fixed; when the total asset value is adjusted consistently for the same writedown, the break-even uniform haircut is approximately 19.39% (conditional assumptions). This is a material valuation/eligibility dependency.

(2) If the entire customer-relationship category qualifies and its $5.265B book value is instead replaced by an independently supportable current market value V, while every other item is provisionally held at book=market, the condition becomes ($25.228B + V) / ($89.547B + V) ≥ 30%, giving a hypothetical break-even V = $2.337B (44.39% of recorded book value). This number is an algebraic threshold, not a valuation opinion. Both scenarios assume developed technology and trademarks are qualifying rights at the stated values.

Consistent revaluation sensitivities: With all $15.101B acquired intangibles hypothetically treated as eligible and their value cut 10%, both qualifying numerator and total-asset denominator change, giving 31.06%. A 20% haircut gives 29.93%; the break-even uniform haircut is approximately 19.39%. These illustrative assumptions do not constitute actual 2026 rights market values. No independently reliable public market-value appraisal establishing a confirmed SS21 pass was located in this targeted review.

Targeted disposition: CONDITIONAL BOOK-VALUE PASS, FINAL TRADABILITY NOT VERIFIED. AMD's reported business composition does not justify failing the test solely on physical assets. Equally, its acquisition accounting does not justify automatically certifying a pass. The single decisive evidence request is a Shariah-acceptable classification and contemporaneous valuation of the underlying contractual component of customer relationships, together with valuation evidence for the technology and trademarks assumed in these break-even calculations. The overall SHARIAH REVIEW REQUIRED verdict stays unchanged; separately unresolved equity/warrant gains and purification assumptions still require consideration.
