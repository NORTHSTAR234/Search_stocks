Executive summary

AMD's core semiconductor business is generally permissible. Its 2026-09-26 interest-bearing borrowing and conservative cash/investment deposit ratios are far below AAOIFI SS21's 30% limits. Identified positive investment/interest income of US$975m is 2.773% of US$35,166m nine-month operating revenue, but net investment gains are not a gross all-source prohibited-income cap and specific financial contracts need classification. Qualifying physical assets equal 16.234% of reported book assets; developed technology and trademarks lift a conditional proxy to 26.608%, and adding all customer relationships yields 32.162% on book values only. Customer-right eligibility and market values are not verified, so overall Shariah review remains required, not a demonstrated failure. Separately, the owner's Nov 2026 RizqInvests snapshot labels AMD Neutral, without a BDS badge.

Missing evidence to decide: (1) gross Shariah classification and cap for relevant investment/financial gains and contracts; (2) legal eligibility and sufficient market values for qualifying rights/benefits under SS21 3/19.

A. Business activities and prohibited components

AMD sells semiconductors and computing platforms in Data Center, Client and Gaming, and Embedded divisions. The manufacturing, chip-design and enterprise-computing core is ordinarily permissible. The 2026 filing also discloses interest-bearing corporate notes, an interest-bearing US$100m loan receivable to an equity investee, long-term investments (including equity securities and warrants), foreign-exchange hedges, credit/lease guarantees for business partners, and customer-linked warrants. These do not transform chip manufacturing into a verified impermissible principal business, but their specific legal nature matters to ancillary-transaction and income screening.   • Reporting-date market capitalization is based on a nearest-prior-session equity price and reported quarterly share count; latest close sensitivity does not alter the debt/deposit outcomes.
  • US-GAAP asset carrying amounts are not the same as legally eligible current market values under SS21. Treat technology, product trademarks and customer relationships separately.
