A. Business activity

King Yuan Electronics performs semiconductor wafer probing, integrated-circuit testing, final testing, packaging and related engineering services. These are intrinsically permissible industrial activities under AAOIFI's primary-business screen. Taiwan Stock Exchange lists it as a semiconductor company and states an integrated-circuit manufacturing, testing and trade objective. Primary business: PASS.

Its full corporate memorandum/articles were not independently authenticated for explicit financing objectives. Public activity descriptions do not establish an impermissible primary objective. Investment holdings and any associated dividends remain to be classified separately.

F. Purification

Full AAOIFI purification: NOT CALCULABLE from verified public disclosure components. Reported nine-month interest income is NT$97,781,000. The assumed 30 September period-end ordinary share count is 1,283,882,318 shares. The identifiable interest-only component is:

NT$97,781,000 ÷ 1,283,882,318 shares = NT$0.076160 per share

This NT$0.07616/share is a conditional identified-interest-only amount, NOT the total mandatory purification. Other prohibited income and the correct accounting treatment of investee income and net gains remain unresolved. Nine-month cumulative results can overlap previously purified quarters; do not double-count them. If holdings at the relevant financial-period end are known, an illustrative interest-component amount is NT$0.07616 × shares held, but no verified complete amount can be presented.

SS21 §3/4/6 ties purification to prohibited income attributable to shares owned at financial-period end, irrespective of whether dividends were distributed. It is not a generic dividend-purification-percentage method.

G. Data quality and unresolved issues

  1. Full prohibited-income bridge missing. Other income NT$298.545m, net other gains/losses NT$533.701m and equity-investee income NT$38.533m require gross-component and Shariah category reconciliation; their NET presentation cannot establish the maximum prohibited income. The identified interest NT$97.781m alone is 0.292% of NT$33.476bn operating revenue, not a complete numerator.
  2. Investment holdings. NT$19.596bn FVOCI securities must be reviewed for whether they represent equity, bonds or other instruments and whether related income is prohibited. They were not classified as deposits without support.
  3. Deposits. Bank account interest-bearing/noninterest-bearing splits, restricted deposits and other financial assets require note-level confirmation even though a gross-cash proxy is safely below the ratio limit.
  4. Assets and charter. Market values of tangible rights and full constitutional objectives were not separately certified; book proxies are supportive but not conclusive.
  5. Interim reviewer qualification. The Q3 interim review carries a procedural qualified conclusion involving nonmaterial subsidiaries/investee statements not separately reviewed; it is not a claim that the entire filing is false, but lowers absolute certainty.

H. AAOIFI guidance and decision

INSUFFICIENT DATA — financial tests mostly favorable, but full Shariah clearance not established. There is no evidence of a prohibited primary business or a 30% borrowing-cap failure. The precise 5% prohibited-income test remains unresolved and must be reconciled before calling 2449.TWSE AAOIFI-COMPLIANT.
