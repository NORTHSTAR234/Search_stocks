H. AAOIFI guidance

The business activity, borrowing, deposit ceiling and tangible-asset screens point favorably toward AAOIFI eligibility. The official classification of this evidence-driven screening is nevertheless SHARIAH REVIEW REQUIRED because two important Shariah matters need expert interpretation: gross interest/other prohibited-income reconciliation and the substantial physically settled commodity contracts accounted for as derivatives. This is not a finding that all such contracts are impermissible, nor a financial buy/sell decision.
