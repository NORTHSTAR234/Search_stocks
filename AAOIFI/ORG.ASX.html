E. Tradability / asset composition

FY26 PP&E book value A$6,290m ÷ total assets A$20,779m = 30.27%, narrowly above the ≥30% mixed-assets benchmark. Inventories A$268m increase the combined book amount to 31.56%. However Origin notes right-of-use assets of A$1,080m within PP&E; if they are excluded entirely as physical property, the PP&E ratio drops to 25.07% (or 26.36% including inventory). AAOIFI refers more broadly to tangible assets, benefits and rights; some right-of-use rights and intangible rights may qualify, but their admissible valuation has not been verified. Tradability: SHARIAH REVIEW REQUIRED rather than declaring a definitive pass or fail from narrow book-value percentages.

F. Purification

The FY26 report explicitly recognises A$29m interest earned from third parties, plus APLNG's A$25m Origin-attributable interest component shown in the equity-accounted JV financial summary. Treat the sum A$54m as a provisional identified interest component requiring purification, not a verified complete prohibited-income total; care is required to exclude any intercompany/elimination duplication and resolve other associates' returns.

Identified component/share = A$54,000,000 ÷ 1,717,732,844 closing ordinary shares ≈ A$0.0314368/share ≈ 3.14 Australian cents/share.

Identified provisional lower-bound illustration: 1000 period-end shares × A$0.0314368 ≈ A$31.44. Exact AAOIFI purification remains NOT CALCULABLE because complete prohibited income (including investees and fund/security returns) has not been established. This is not the commonly used dividend × impure-income-percentage shortcut.

The stored SS21 research basis links purification to ownership at the financial-year end (here 30 June 2026), regardless of dividend payment. Share-count convention (period-end 1,717,732,844 versus basic weighted average 1,719,898,967), JV netting and potential restatements need confirmation. If you did not own shares at FY26 year-end, don't automatically treat the illustrative per-share amount as your own liability.
