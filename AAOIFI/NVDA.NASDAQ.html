Executive summary

NVIDIA's main business is permissible and its AAOIFI borrowing and deposit ratios are far below the 30% limits. The unresolved issue is the Shariah classification of very large equity-investment gains. Depending on how those gains are classified, prohibited income could remain below 5% or rise above it, so a clean AAOIFI-compliant verdict is not yet defensible.

A. Business activity

NVIDIA's primary activities are semiconductors, AI accelerators, graphics, networking and computing platforms. These are not intrinsically prohibited businesses under the AAOIFI share-screening framework.

C. Accounting bridge

Conventional interest-bearing debt is included in the borrowing numerator. Cash, debt securities and equity investments are kept separate rather than automatically collapsed into an AAOIFI deposit numerator. The material uncertainty is the Shariah status of gains from equity securities.

D. Market-capitalisation convention

AAOIFI SS21 specifies market capitalisation for the borrowing and deposit tests but does not specify a 24- or 36-month averaging window. Stock_screener therefore uses market capitalisation at or close to the financial-position date as an explicitly disclosed implementation convention and tests sensitivity where material.

E. Tradability / asset composition

The separate mixed-asset tradability test is not reliably reproducible from public GAAP book values alone because SS21 refers to the value/market value of tangible assets, benefits and rights. This remains a Shariah-review item.

F. Purification

Confirmed interest income alone implies a minimum identifiable purification of about US$0.04295 per share for the first six months using 24.147B shares. This is a minimum, not a final amount, because the investment-gain classification remains unresolved.

G. Data quality and unresolved issues

The unresolved investment gains are large enough to change the prohibited-income verdict. Stock_screener therefore does not treat missing classification as zero.

H. AAOIFI guidance

SHARIAH REVIEW REQUIRED. Core business, borrowing and deposit tests are comfortably within limits, but the underlying investments producing material gains need classification before a final AAOIFI verdict and purification amount can be stated.
