A. Company and principal business

NVIDIA Corporation, Delaware issuer, trades ordinary shares on NASDAQ (not an ADR). Its business comprises Compute & Networking and Graphics, principally AI accelerators, data-center computing and networks, gaming and visualization. Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue was US$96.221bn; Data Center represented US$89.0bn, reflecting a predominantly technology rather than conventional-finance business.

The public disclosures include securities investments, conventional interest-bearing notes, finance-related partner arrangements and dual-use products sold through channels that can reach military customers. These require review for ancillary prohibited dealings; they do not by themselves establish that NVIDIA's main business is prohibited. Legal corporate objects and individual sensitive customer contracts were not fully verified.

Sources: SEC Q2 10-Q; audited 2026 10-K.

F. Purification — SS21-style amount

The six months ended 26 July 2026 disclose US$1.037bn of interest income. An identifiable minimum from disclosed interest alone equals US$1,037m / 24,147m = US$0.04295 per share. For 100 shares held at the applicable period end, this is approximately US$4.29 of interest-only purification, before the unresolved income components.

If all US$23,707m net equity gains also required purification, the *scenario subtotal for those two known classes* would be approximately US$1.02472 per share, or US$102.47 for 100 shares. This is not a verified upper bound for all prohibited income, nor a final payable amount. The income period is interim, share count is period-end, and an annual final figure awaits full-year audited disclosures and Shariah classification. No dividend-ratio shortcut is used. Sold-before-period-end holdings are not adjudicated here.
