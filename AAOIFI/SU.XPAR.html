A. Business activity

Primary activity: PASS. Schneider Electric SE manufactures, develops and sells energy management, electrical distribution, automation and data-centre equipment, controls, industrial software and services. These activities are not inherently prohibited by AAOIFI SS21.

Article 2 of Schneider Electric SE's July 2026 Memorandum and Articles of Association states electrical equipment, industrial controls, building automation, data-centre infrastructure and related intellectual-property objectives. It also permits financial and asset transactions related to those industries, but does not expressly make interest-based lending a corporate purpose. The latest publicly described company activities are Energy Management and Industrial Automation.

H1 2026 revenue was €21,226 million. The company's July 2026 investor presentation separates operations into products (47%), systems (35%) and software/services (18%) of Q2 sales, not intrinsically prohibited sectors. Any incidental financial income remains subject to the separate tests.
