E. Growth opportunities

AI accelerators need high-bandwidth memory, fast DDR5 and server/storage capacity; Micron's HBM qualification and DRAM/NAND product mix can benefit from hyperscaler demand. Management highlighted structural supply tightness, growing advanced memory capacity and better margins in its September results. Upside depends on realized customer orders, sustained pricing and capital discipline; HBM competitors and expanding supply constrain durability.
