Why the price moved

A. Why did Micron fall despite record memory earnings?

The company is earning record profits, but investors are reassessing AI-memory demand, export-policy risk and whether today's extraordinary margins will last.

◈
Semiconductor stocks sold off together.

Micron fell 7.62% from the 13 November close to 16 November, while the SOXX semiconductor ETF fell 5.41% over the identical completed-session window. MU underperformed by 2.21 percentage points. This corroborates broad sector selling, but does not establish the cause of MU's extra decline. MU prices · 16 Nov · SOXX prices · 16 Nov · CONFIRMED MARKET FACT

⇢
Reported US export restrictions raised demand concerns.

A 16 November financial-news report linked the selloff to potential new US controls on AI-related technology exports to China. If computing deployments are delayed, advanced memory shipments could also be affected. Exact final rule text, implementation and MU-specific revenue exposure were not independently verified for this report. Motley Fool · 16 Nov 2026 · REPORTED / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▤
Record profits create unusually demanding expectations.

Micron reported fiscal-2026 revenue of $133.19bn and GAAP net income of $84.97bn. Exceptional margins benefit the share price only while investors believe they will persist; any hint of supply normalisation can trigger fast valuation compression. There is no evidence here that 16 November's move proves a confirmed earnings deterioration. Issuer · 30 Sep 2026 · FY2026 10-K · CONFIRMED EARNINGS / PLAUSIBLE PRICE DRIVER

⚖
A DRAM competition lawsuit adds unquantified risk.

A 16 November news article reported that an alleged DRAM price-collusion lawsuit had advanced. This is a reported allegation, not a finding of wrongdoing, and its potential financial impact is not established. It is a possible contributor rather than a verified explanation for the day's full move. Investing.com · 16 Nov 2026 · REPORTED / NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED
