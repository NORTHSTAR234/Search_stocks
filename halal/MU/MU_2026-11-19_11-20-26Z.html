E. Catalysts and investment thesis

  • Growth: HBM/DRAM demand from large AI/cloud deployments and longer supply agreements can sustain pricing if volume commitments materialize.
  • Product mix: HBM, data-center DRAM and SSD mix may lift margin relative to lower-value commodity chips; execution and manufacturing yield must be demonstrated.
  • Capital allocation: FY2026 net cash and raised share-repurchase authorization offer optionality but cash deployment is not guaranteed.
  • Failure modes: Memory oversupply, faster Chinese supply expansion, restrictions on exports/customer demand, overcapacity, depreciation and AI capex slowing.
