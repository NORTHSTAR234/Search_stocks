E. Growth opportunities

  • High-bandwidth memory for AI accelerators: Micron ships HBM4 in volume and works toward HBM4E in 2027. Higher-bandwidth memory content per accelerator and strong contract demand support revenue and profitability, conditional on yield/capacity.
  • Data Center DRAM and SSDs: Micron's Core Data Center business reported $18B revenue FQ4, with high demand for enterprise/cloud products; storage for retrieval and KV-cache extends use cases.
  • Strategic customer agreements and high-density systems: Micron's long-term customer commitments plus advanced DDR5 modules and server LPDDR could support revenue visibility. However, contracted volume does not eliminate the risk of future repricing or demand shifts.

Three main investment risks and invalidation signals

  • Memory oversupply and price normalization: current exceptionally high gross margin and FY2027 EPS estimates could collapse as supply catches up or customers resist higher prices.
  • Large manufacturing investment: 2027 capex commitments plus HBM yield/technology execution can strain future FCF even from a strong cash balance.
  • Concentration and geopolitics: dependence on large AI customers, export restrictions, China exposure and competing suppliers may limit orders/pricing. Fundamental thesis weakens if guidance cuts repeat or contract conversion fails.
