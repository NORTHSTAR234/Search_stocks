E. Growth opportunities

HBM and AI data-center server deployments require substantial high-performance memory. Micron competes with SK hynix and Samsung in DRAM and NAND; AI qualification and memory content per server can grow volumes. Customer demand, pricing power, process technology and manufacturing expansion may support 2027 results, but unit demand and signed commitments are not equivalent to proven profit at projected prices. Capital intensity, electricity, yields and fixed fab costs can absorb revenue gains. Micron FY2027 Q1 management outlook.
