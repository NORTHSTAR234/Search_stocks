1. Micron Technology investment analysis

Why the price moved

A. Why did Micron fall even after reporting record memory profits?

Record profits can coexist with falling shares when investors fear that today’s extraordinary memory earnings will not last or price an AI-investment slowdown before results change.

↘
Chipmakers sold off together as AI expectations cooled.

On 16 November Micron fell -7.62%, compared with -5.41% for SOXX across the same completed US regular session. MU underperformed by 2.21 percentage points. The common sell-off is confirmed; a single specific cause of Micron’s additional decline is not. MU · 16 Nov SOXX · 16 Nov CONFIRMED FACT / CAUSE NOT ESTABLISHED

◈
Record memory profits bring demanding growth expectations.

Micron reported fiscal-2026 revenue of $133.19 billion, GAAP net income of $84.97 billion and diluted EPS of $74.33 on 30 September. High present profitability supports cash flow, but investors may discount memory-cycle normalisation before reported results turn lower. This is a plausible valuation mechanism, not a proven explanation for the 16 November decline. Micron · 30 Sep 2026 CONFIRMED RESULTS / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▤
Large capacity investment increases sensitivity to demand.

Micron reported FY2026 net capital expenditure of $27.37 billion and adjusted free cash flow of $62.31 billion. Expansion can support future HBM shipments but becomes a risk if AI orders, memory prices or customer qualifications disappoint. The filings confirm spending; they do not identify investment as the direct cause of the one-day share move. Micron · 30 Sep 2026 SEC 10-K · Oct 2026 CONFIRMED FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

△
Policy uncertainty could disturb demand and market sentiment.

Contemporaneous reporting linked broader semiconductor selling to policy and export uncertainty, while technology stocks declined across the sector. A restriction could affect customers or the economics of AI equipment deployment, but no issuer-confirmed MU-specific sales loss from a new rule has been established in this research. The exact causal share remains unknown. MarketWatch · 16 Nov 2026 REPORTED / UNVERIFIED MU-SPECIFIC IMPACT
