E. Growth opportunities

  • Cross-border travel and commerce: higher transaction and cross-border volume may support payment fees and operating leverage.
  • Visa Direct and new payment flows: real-time money movement and B2B services expand addressable flows beyond retail card payments.
  • Value-added services: tokenization, authentication, risk/identity tools and advisory offerings increase revenue per payment-network relationship.

Growth must translate into durable EPS and cash-flow growth rather than simply rising processed volume.

F. Risks and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS: 28 JANUARY 2027 — THIRD-PARTY ESTIMATE, NOT ISSUER-CONFIRMED. Consensus for FY2027 Q1 adjusted EPS roughly $3.63. Watch revenue, cross-border and processed-transaction growth, service margins, capital allocation and updates on payment-network litigation.

  • Regulation / lawsuits: interchange and debit-network competition actions could reduce pricing power or increase costs.
  • Consumer and travel slowdown: recessions and FX changes could suppress processed and cross-border volumes.
  • Multiple compression and competing rails: wallets, account-to-account payments and other alternatives challenge growth expectations.

Thesis invalidation: sustained deterioration of core growth, margin or network economics; an adverse regulatory change with lasting financial impact. Stop levels alone are not automatic sell orders.
