E. Growth opportunities

  1. iPhone upgrade cycle: FY2026 iPhone sales increased 21% to $254.10B. Device design and AI features could sustain premium mix, subject to demand and competition.
  2. Services economics: $123.30B of FY2026 sales (+13%) supplies recurring, attractive-margin revenue across advertising, cloud and subscriptions, offset by possible regulatory pressure.
  3. Mac and integrated AI ecosystem: Mac sales rose 13% in FY2026; on-device AI and software integration may drive retention and future device adoption, although incremental AI profitability is not independently quantified.
