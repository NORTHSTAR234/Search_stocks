E · Three issuer-specific growth opportunities

1. Edge AI and non-PC equipment: Management says the non-PC business may exceed 10% of 2026 sales and targeted further significant growth over future years; these are management aims, not assured revenues. Growth could diversify earnings away from consumer gaming. MoneyDJ 18 Nov

2. Premium gaming hardware and higher ASP: Supply-constrained graphics products and better high-end mix benefited margins in reported 2026 results. Raising price can preserve revenue per unit, but reduced buyer demand and rising memory cost may offset that advantage. MoneyDJ 19 Nov

3. Enterprise/industrial systems: MSI markets AI servers, industrial computers and local/edge inference hardware; more server and industrial demand would diversify consumer-PC exposure. However, management says it is not yet a scale manufacturer to large hyperscale cloud buyers, so assuming a large CSP-AI boom would be unwarranted. MSI reported strategy, 18 Nov
