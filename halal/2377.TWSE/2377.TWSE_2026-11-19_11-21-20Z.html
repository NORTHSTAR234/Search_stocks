A · Why the price moved

Why did MSI fall harder than the Taiwan market on 19 November? A broad selloff coincided with a same-day warning about the PC supply chain. Neither market direction nor the interview alone proves the allocation of all trades; the price decline had more than one plausible driver.

↘ Management warned of constrained 2027 PC, gaming and board shipments.

In a report dated 19 November 09:00 Taipei, MSI's general manager described AI-related chip supply competition and memory price increases, citing possible 10–20% first-half 2027 shipment contraction. MSI plans higher average selling prices and new growth products. Lower shipments can weaken fixed-cost leverage, while ASP increases may defend revenue—but actual margins remain unknown.
