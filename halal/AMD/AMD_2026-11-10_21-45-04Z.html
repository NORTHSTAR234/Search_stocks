E. Growth opportunities

  • Instinct GPU acceleration: AI server deployments and MI450 scaling could increase accelerator revenue, as seen in 97% Data Center Q3 growth.
  • EPYC CPU share gains: cloud and enterprise server refreshes broaden revenue beyond GPUs and can improve the Data Center product mix.
  • Helios, Venice and AI PCs: systems-level AI platforms and client product refreshes can expand addressable demand, but the revenue conversion is execution-dependent.

AMD's Q4 guidance of approximately $17B revenue (+/-3%) at ~55% non-GAAP gross margin reinforces management's near-term demand outlook.
