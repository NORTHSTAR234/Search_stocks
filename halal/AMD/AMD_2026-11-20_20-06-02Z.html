Why the price moved

A. Why did AMD slump when data-center sales were accelerating?

Strong chips revenue has not removed investors' worries about regulatory uncertainty, cash conversion or the high expectations embedded in AMD's share price.

⚖
New allegations prompted questions about export controls.

On 19 November Reuters reported that a short-seller-aligned publication questioned AMD links to a Chinese server supplier. AMD stated it complies with applicable US export-control laws. The critical report was published, but alleged misconduct has not been verified; it is not possible to assign the price fall entirely to the report. ↔
AI-sector investors faced pressure and potential rotation.

A 19 November Wall Street Journal market account relayed research about investors selling some AI stocks amid the Anthropic public listing. That is a reported flow explanation, not verified AMD investor-level sales. Higher risk aversion could pressure AMD's valuation, but an individual causal share is not measurable. Wall Street Journal · 19 Nov 2026 · REPORTED ANALYSIS; PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▥
AMD fell considerably more than semiconductor peers.

AMD fell -7.72% over the 18-to-19 November completed regular sessions, while semiconductor ETF SOXX fell -2.75%. The 4.97 percentage-point difference suggests company-specific or positioning risk beyond shared sector weakness. The return comparison establishes divergence, not why each shareholder sold. SOXX history · 19 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED MARKET DATA; CAUSE UNPROVEN
