E. Growth opportunities

  1. Instinct and Helios rack-scale AI systems: expanded demand for training/inference compute and AMD's direct relationships with frontier AI labs create new revenue scale; gross margin, ROCm software adoption and supply commitments remain critical.
  2. EPYC Venice and server share: 5th-generation EPYC selected as the host CPU for Google Cloud TPU 8tx, underscoring demand for AMD even in systems using customer-designed AI accelerators; sales and mix can support high-margin data centre growth.
  3. Anthropic / hyperscaler deployment pipeline: July 2026 agreement to deploy up to 2GW of MI450 systems, first gigawatt planned first half 2027, adds potential multi-year demand visibility. AMD has also committed an equity investment of up to $5B, adding capital exposure; neither contract scale nor future sales are guaranteed.
