E. Growth case and the limits of the thesis

AMD competes in server CPUs and AI accelerators; the Data Center division's 97% Q3 sales growth is evidence of strong current demand. The investment case depends on AI deployments converting into profitable recurring chip sales with sustainable cash flow, not simply on total addressable market claims. The company describes its future MI450/Helios platform roadmap, but adoption and margins remain execution-dependent. AMD Q3 · 3 Nov.

Potential positives include chip demand diversification and improved product mix; material uncertainties include NVIDIA competition, advanced packaging capacity, export-policy changes, enterprise spending cycles, inventory conversion, acquisition dilution and unusually high volatility. There is no issuer-confirmed long-range share-price forecast.
