Section E · Growth and quality of earnings

Investment thesis and its vulnerabilities

Infineon is positioned in high-voltage power conversion and efficient AI data-centre electricity distribution, automotive microcontrollers and industrial power. Potential multi-year tailwinds include AI rack power density, electric vehicle semiconductors and grid upgrades. These need to deliver higher yields, sustained orders, a better mix and real cash flow. Risks include China/EV demand cycles, intense competition, capacity spending, FX (company assumptions €/$1.15), power-tech pricing, debt and large earlier valuation swings.
