E. Growth opportunities

1. Mounjaro and Zepbound: Strong diabetes/obesity volume and wider international coverage support revenue expansion, but economics depend on reimbursement and capacity.

2. Foundayo oral treatment: The 9 Nov ATTAIN-OSA results showed up to 43.8% fewer apnea-hypopnea events in the relevant Phase 3 group; planned FDA filing by end-2026 could broaden the label. Approval and uptake are not assured.

3. Broader therapies and capacity: oncology, immunology, neuroscience plus new U.S. manufacturing investment diversify growth, though expensive development and execution risk may limit near-term cash conversion.
