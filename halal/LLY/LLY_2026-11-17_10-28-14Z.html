Why the price moved

Revenue has expanded quickly, but a premium share price depends on sustainable profits and investor expectations—not revenue alone.

Lilly outperformed by 1.10 percentage points; broader risk aversion is a plausible contributor, not a demonstrated explanation for LLY's particular fall. In Q3 2026, revenue rose 39%, while reported EPS rose only 16% to $7.22 and non-GAAP EPS rose 7% to $7.48.
