E. Growth opportunities

  1. Mounjaro / Zepbound: domestic and global obesity/diabetes penetration can sustain a large multi-year volume runway if manufacturing and coverage expand.
  2. Foundayo (orforglipron): 9 Nov Phase 3 sleep-apnea result showed up to 44% reductions in interruptions; an additional U.S. filing is planned by year-end 2026. An oral option could improve convenience and broaden eligibility, subject to regulatory approval, prescribing and access.
  3. Portfolio breadth and manufacturing: oncology, immunology, neuroscience, retatrutide and capacity investments can diversify future growth, while new acquisitions can raise investment needs and earnings volatility.
