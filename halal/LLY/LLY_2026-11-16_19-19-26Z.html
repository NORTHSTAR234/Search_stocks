Why the price moved

A. Why has Lilly's share price climbed despite expensive expectations?

Strong drug demand supports Lilly's earnings, but investors also pay a high premium for growth that must continue.

↗
Obesity-drug sales are delivering rapid growth.

On 29 October, Lilly reported third-quarter revenue of $24.397 billion, 39% higher year on year, driven mainly by Mounjaro and Zepbound. Strong demand supports the share-price recovery, but this earnings release does not prove what caused any specific day’s trading. Lilly · 29 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT

▤
Earnings rose more slowly than sales.

Q3 reported diluted EPS rose about 16% to $7.22, versus revenue up 39%; acquired research and development charges were a major difference. This is an important warning that rapid sales growth is not always equivalent to faster accounting profit growth. SEC 10-Q · 29 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT

✚
New obesity results broaden the growth story.

At ObesityWeek 14–17 November, Lilly presented results and patient evidence across Zepbound, Foundayo, retatrutide and EloraTZP. The pipeline may support long-term adoption, but clinical promise is not the same as assured reimbursement, approval or future profits. Lilly · 14 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT; PRICE CONTRIBUTION UNPROVEN

A premium valuation can magnify disappointments.

Near $1,442, Lilly trades at roughly 40.6 times the midpoint of management's FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance ($35.50). A new analyst 12-month consensus target near $1,469 offers little near-term cushion at this quote; this is a valuation risk, not evidence of a specific trade cause.
