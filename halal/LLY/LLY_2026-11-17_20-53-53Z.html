Why the price moved

A. Why did Lilly pause despite booming obesity-drug demand?

Its sales and outlook have risen sharply, but the shares are already pricing in years of strong profits; the latest daily retreat was smaller than the wider market's.

↗
The October earnings surprise reset growth expectations.

On 29 October Lilly reported third-quarter revenue of $24.397bn, up 39%, and lifted its 2026 sales guide to $91–92bn. The 29 October close climbed to $1,373.70 from $1,288.30 on 28 October, consistent with investors repricing better demand; this alignment does not isolate the announcement's entire causal effect. Issuer · 29 Oct · SEC 10-Q · 29 Oct · CONFIRMED RESULTS / PLAUSIBLE PRICE CONTRIBUTOR

≋
Sales are growing faster than reported profits.

Third-quarter net profit rose 16% to $6.451bn, much slower than the 39% revenue increase; EPS rose 16% to $7.22. Lilly disclosed acquired R&D charges and lower realised prices, so the premium valuation depends on converting continued patient demand into lasting earnings rather than only headline prescriptions. Issuer · 29 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT / VALUATION INFERENCE

◈
New trial findings offer opportunities and execution risks.

On 14 November the company outlined ObesityWeek results for Zepbound, Foundayo, EloraTZP and retatrutide, including an EloraTZP Phase 2 arm reporting 23.3% average weight loss at 48 weeks. These may enlarge future treatment opportunities, but early-stage results and valuation expectations do not prove they caused the 16 November price move. E. Growth opportunities

Cardiometabolic scale. High-volume Mounjaro/Zepbound growth and more indications could expand revenue; pressure on realised prices remains a counterweight (29 Oct earnings).

Next-generation oral and injectable candidates. Foundayo, EloraTZP and retatrutide clinical outcomes could broaden populations and conditions; final trial, safety, approval and access milestones are still uncertain (14 Nov clinical preview).

Manufacturing and oncology diversification. The company is funding capacity and development programs, with recently announced Calytrix acquisition terms and Stipple collaboration. Headline milestone ceilings are conditional, not booked revenue; near-term diversification contribution should not be overstated (Reuters · 16 Nov).
