A strong franc reduces reported sales growth.

In Sika’s 23 October results, local-currency sales grew 4.8% for nine months, but reported CHF sales grew 1.6%, including a −3.2 percentage-point FX effect. This translation drag can limit CHF earnings expectations; it is a confirmed operating fact but only a plausible price contributor. CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR · Sika · 23 Oct 2026

⌁
China and Asia construction activity remain uneven.

Sika’s Asia-Pacific sales declined approximately 2.3% in local currencies during the nine months, versus EMEA growth near 8.3%. A weak regional construction cycle could undermine forecasts and a premium valuation, but the company did not establish it as the cause of Friday’s trading. CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR · Sika · 23 Oct 2026

≋
Cash generation has lagged accounting profits.

First-half operating free cash flow was CHF139.6 million, below the prior CHF181.9 million, even as nine-month EBITDA reached about CHF1.68 billion. Lower short-term conversion matters for debt resilience and acquisitions; the date-specific effect on stock price is unverified. CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR · Sika · 28 Jul 2026

Margin resilience argues against a purely negative interpretation.

Sika’s nine-month EBITDA margin held at 19.2%, while management reiterated full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 19.0–19.5% and its savings initiative. Those facts counter the idea that demand or profitability has uniformly collapsed, although guidance is not guaranteed. CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / COUNTEREVIDENCE · Sika · 23 Oct 2026
