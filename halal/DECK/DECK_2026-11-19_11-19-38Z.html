E. Long-term growth opportunities and competition

Growth path

HOKA performance running footwear, geographic penetration, product innovation and UGG's premium lifestyle franchise offer separate demand drivers. Both brands still grew in the latest quarter; international expansion can offset some US headwinds. Watch revenue mix and full-price/direct-to-consumer sell-through, not just promotional volume.

Competitive threat

On Holding, Nike, Lululemon and discounted footwear alternatives can pressure pricing, distribution and consumer attention. HOKA's high-single-digit and UGG's low-single-digit FY2027 growth assumptions are far more modest than earlier growth expectations. Inventory and promotional cadence matter as much as sales growth.
