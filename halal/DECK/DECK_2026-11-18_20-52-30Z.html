Why the price moved

A. Why is DECK falling even though its headline earnings are improving?

Deckers' latest quarterly revenue was higher, but the full-year sales outlook was cut; the market may be reassessing growth and repeatable earnings, rather than celebrating higher reported EPS.

📉
Deckers reduced its full-year sales outlook.

Fiscal 2027 revenue guidance fell to $5.700–5.725bn from prior approximately $5.86–5.91bn. This is a confirmed change in management's outlook but is not proof of the exact cause of the later 16–17 November market move. Issuer filing · 22 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT / PLAUSIBLE PRICE CONTRIBUTOR.

🛍️
HOKA growth slowed amid heavier footwear discounting.

HOKA quarterly sales +6.8% to $677.2m and UGG +3.5% to $786.4m, but promotional competition increases pressure on full-price sales. Management still reported positive sales growth at both brands, which is important counterevidence to permanent demand impairment. Issuer · 22 Oct · Reuters · 22 Oct · CONFIRMED RESULTS / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR.

💵
Tariff refunds lift earnings without recurring sales.

Guided EPS $7.80–$7.95 includes approximately $0.52 from IEEPA tariff net refunds; for valuation, illustrative normalized midpoint EPS is $7.355 after deducting the stated benefit. The $7.355 normalized midpoint is my mechanical valuation illustration, not an issuer-issued earnings target. Issuer · 22 Oct · CONFIRMED REFUND / ANALYST MODEL.

E. Long-term growth thesis and competition

HOKA performance footwear and UGG premium lifestyle footwear provide distinct end-markets and international opportunities. Management continues to emphasise full-price brand health instead of aggressive promotions. Risk: if shoppers defer purchases or rivals discount further, top-line growth may slow before costs adjust. Track HOKA growth (fiscal-2027 high-single-digit guidance), UGG low-single-digit guidance, wholesale/digital mix, inventory and working-capital cash conversion; do not rely on share buybacks to mask operating deterioration.
