E. Growth opportunities

  • AI Ethernet/optics infrastructure: FY2026 hyperscaler AI orders reached $9.3B, while Cisco targets about $7.5B AI infrastructure revenue for FY2027 versus about $4B in FY2026. This can expand scale but is less attractive if hardware mix dilutes gross margins.
  • Campus and enterprise replacement: Q4 FY2026 networking orders grew 40% year-on-year. Switching, routing and power/bandwidth refresh could translate orders into multi-quarter product and attached services sales.
  • Security, Splunk and observability: Cross-selling and subscriptions can deepen client relationships, diversify revenue and support recurring gross profit, though integration execution matters.
