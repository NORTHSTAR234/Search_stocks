  • AI Ethernet/optics: FY2026 hyperscaler AI orders reached $9.3B, and FY2027 AI-related revenue is expected at ~$7.5B. Incremental growth is meaningful but hardware margins must be protected.

  • Security and observability: Splunk cross-selling, security and software subscriptions can add recurring revenue and deepen Cisco's installed-base monetisation.
