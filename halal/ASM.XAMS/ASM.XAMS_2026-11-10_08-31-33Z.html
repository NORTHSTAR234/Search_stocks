E. Growth opportunities

  • 2nm and 1.4nm logic: new transistor designs increase deposition requirements; ASM expects higher served-addressable market and share at 1.4nm.
  • AI infrastructure demand: advanced foundry investment and memory capacity support deposition-equipment orders and pricing resilience.
  • Memory and recovery markets: management sees strong memory growth and a strengthening power/analog/wafer recovery in 2027, broadening demand beyond one end market.

F. Risks and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS: 23 February 2027, 18:00 CET — CONFIRMED by ASM's calendar. The 19 November Morgan Stanley conference and 1 December industry conference may provide additional commentary.

Key near-term guideposts: ASM's Q4 2026 revenue outlook is €1.230bn ±5% at constant currencies, with management projecting 2027 revenue above €5.7bn. Assess order quality, shipment timing, 52%-area margins, backlog conversion, China demand, and whether supplier constraints limit output.

  • China export controls: potential US/Dutch rules may restrict tools and customer demand.
  • Equipment-cycle risk: AI capex and leading-edge foundry buildouts may be delayed or concentrated among very large customers.
  • High volatility and execution: the stock dropped hard in late October/early November even after strong results; valuation is sensitive to guidance or sentiment changes.

Thesis invalidation: management cuts its 2027 growth trajectory materially, loses deposition competitiveness at advanced nodes, or margins deteriorate persistently. These are more important to a holder than a single technical stop.
