E. Growth opportunities

Advanced foundry nodes (2nm / 1.4nm) use more deposition steps and ASM expects a larger served market at 1.4nm with stronger share. AI infrastructure and high-bandwidth memory increase equipment intensity, with Q3 sales to memory customers rising. Spares and services revenue reached €223.1m in Q3 (+32% reported YoY); new Flowable Carbon technology expands exposure beyond legacy ALD processes. Management's accelerated 2027 revenue target creates upside if orders and supplier execution remain strong.
