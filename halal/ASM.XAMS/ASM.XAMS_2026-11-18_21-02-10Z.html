Why the price moved

A. Why is ASM rebounding while its growth story remains volatile?

Demand for advanced chip tools remains strong, but investor expectations and export-policy headlines have recently driven large share-price swings.

▤
Chip-equipment buyers welcomed stronger AI demand signals.

On 18 November Reuters reported European semiconductor equipment shares recovering as Nvidia's outlook reassured the sector; ASM closed up 2.60% and the broad AEX finished down 1.01%. The shared rally is consistent with renewed AI demand optimism, although Reuters has not proved Nvidia was the unique cause of ASM's gain. Source · 18 Nov 2026 PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

◈
Export-policy headlines recently unsettled European chip shares.

On 17 November a reported US restriction on overseas AI-compute access coincided with European semiconductor selling and an ASM decline. Policy effects on tool orders, China mix and the semiconductor supply chain are credible risks, but ASM has not attributed a known order cancellation to this particular rule. Source · 17 Nov 2026 REPORTED EVENT / PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

▣
Record sales and margins strengthen the longer-term case.

ASM reported Q3 sales €1,159.4m (+45% reported year-on-year) and adjusted operating margin 33.4%, while management raised its 2027 revenue ambition to over €5.7bn. This is evidence for the underlying thesis, but higher sales do not guarantee investors will pay a higher valuation multiple. Source · 27 Oct 2026 CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / COUNTEREVIDENCE

↗
Profit comparisons and supply constraints complicate the picture.

Q3 reported net profit €308.1m was lower than €384.1m a year earlier, even as adjusted net earnings rose to €315.4m from €206.2m. The 2025 comparison contained a €181.4m impairment reversal on an associate, so an unadjusted EPS fall alone is a misleading sign of operating weakness. Supplier constraints remain a separate risk. Source · 27 Oct 2026 CONFIRMED ISSUER FACT / RISK

Same-session market comparison (17→18 Nov Amsterdam closes): ASM +2.60%, AEX −1.01%, difference +3.61 percentage points. Benchmarks show divergence, not proven causation. AEX after-close recap · 18 Nov; ASM close · 18 Nov.
