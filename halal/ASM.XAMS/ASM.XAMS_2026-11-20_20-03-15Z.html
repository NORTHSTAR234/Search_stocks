A. Why did ASM fall even after record quarterly sales?

Strong customer spending supports earnings, but an expensive cyclical technology share can fall much faster than its local index when investors retreat from AI exposure.

↘
Global technology selling pressured semiconductor shares.

On 20 November Reuters and the Wall Street Journal reported a broad technology-led global equity decline and weakness in European chip-tool names. This made a risk-off sector repricing plausible for ASM. The reporting does not prove a single specific trade caused the €49.60 decline. WSJ · 20 Nov PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

The completed-session ASM close fell 7.20% from €689.20 to €639.60 on 20 November, while the AEX fell 2.36%. ASM's underperformance was 4.84 percentage points on the same date.
