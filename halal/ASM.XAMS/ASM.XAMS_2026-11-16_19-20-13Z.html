Why the price moved

A. Why is ASM falling despite sales growth?

Record wafer-equipment sales do not prevent a stock market sell-off when expectations, valuation and investment sentiment become unsettled.

📉
European AI-equipment valuations are under pressure.

European tech stocks declined on 16 November as investors reconsidered AI-trade earnings expectations. ASM fell 3.57% while the AEX declined 0.60%; semiconductor sentiment is a plausible contributor, not proven individual-stock causation. Reuters · 16 Nov · PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

🧮
Headline net earnings disguise improving core profitability.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 45% reported year on year while reported net profit fell from €384.1m to €308.1m. That comparison includes a €181.4m Q3 2025 associate-investment impairment reversal; Q3 adjusted net earnings instead increased from €206.2m to €315.4m. ASM Q3 · 27 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT

🏭
Rapid equipment demand is stretching the supply chain.

ASM expects Q4 2026 revenue of €1.23bn ±5% at constant currencies, but reported increasingly tight supplier conditions. Delivery bottlenecks could shift revenue recognition, despite demand for 1.4nm/2nm logic and high-bandwidth-memory tools. ASM Q3 · 27 Oct · CONFIRMED RISK

🛡️
Strong growth and repurchases offset the bearish narrative.

ASM expects 2027 constant-currency revenue above €5.7bn and launched a €300m buyback on 11 November. Positive issuer evidence supports the underlying business case but does not mechanically protect its share price. ASM buyback · 11 Nov · CONFIRMED FACT

E. Growth opportunities and thesis monitoring

Advanced logic: ASM has high ALD exposure to 2nm and 1.4nm process steps and says 1.4nm served-addressable market will grow by double digits relative to 2nm with higher share. Memory: high-bandwidth DRAM tooling needs more sophisticated deposition, supporting potential demand. Services: €223.1m Q3 spares/service sales (+32% reported YoY) support a partial recurring revenue cushion. Shareholder returns: the €300m repurchase was initiated on 11 Nov.

Check whether Q4/2027 reported shipment cadence, equipment order demand and margin delivery validate the >€5.7bn plan. The upside case is not guaranteed by AI end-market growth alone, and export restrictions plus suppliers can change the realised order cycle.
