A. Why did ASM rebound while the longer trend remains weak?

Growth and margins are strong, but investors are repricing chip-equipment cycle, export-policy and valuation risks: the stock rose on 18 Nov yet is down about 18.8% from its 14 Oct €824.80 close.

AI spending optimism helped chip equipment recover.

On 18 November, Reuters reported a 1.2% European technology-sector advance after Nvidia's results; ASM was among the gaining chip-equipment names. ASM subsequently closed 2.60% higher, but this simultaneous sector rebound does not prove Nvidia caused every euro of ASM's move. Reuters · 18 Nov PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

◇

On 17 November, reporting linked new U.S. AI-chip access restrictions with selling in European semiconductor stocks, including ASM. The headline could make investors discount future equipment orders, although ASM has not disclosed an order cancellation caused by that specific announcement.
