Why the price moved

A. Why is ASM falling despite stronger sales?

Strong chip-tool growth is real, but investors have also repriced the AI-equipment trade as market sentiment and regulatory concerns worsen.

📉
European semiconductor stocks were already under pressure.

On 16 November, ASM fell 3.57% and the AEX fell 0.60%. Reuters reported weakness in AI-linked European technology and chip names, reflecting market caution. This supports a possible sector contribution but does not prove why ASM received sell orders. Reuters · 16 Nov; AEX · 16 Nov · PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

🌐
New US restrictions add uncertainty for AI supply chains.

On 17 November, Reuters reported tighter US restrictions affecting advanced AI-chip exports and lower European technology stocks. Restrictions on chips are not necessarily direct restrictions on ASM deposition tools; the near-term pathway is sentiment and possible customer spending, not an established ASM order cancellation. Reuters · 17 Nov · CONFIRMED NEWS / PLAUSIBLE COMPANY EFFECT

🧮
Reported earnings look weaker than underlying earnings.

ASM Q3 2026 revenue rose 45% to €1,159.4m, while reported net earnings fell to €308.1m from €384.1m. The year-earlier period included a €181.4m associate impairment reversal; adjusted net earnings instead rose to €315.4m from €206.2m. Treating the GAAP fall as proof of operating decline would be misleading. ASM Q3 · 27 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT

🏭
Tight suppliers threaten delivery timing, not confirmed demand.

ASM projected more than €5.7bn of 2027 revenue at constant currencies, with ALD growth from 2nm/1.4nm. It warned supply-chain conditions were increasingly tight, which could affect shipment timing and margins. This is a verified operating risk, not evidence the company cut guidance. ASM Q3 · 27 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT / PLAUSIBLE RISK

🛡️
Buybacks and strong orders offer a counterweight.

ASM started a €300m repurchase program on 11 November and bought 9,113 shares from 11–13 November at an average €715.55. Strong balance sheet and repurchases support the thesis but did not stop the latest fall; repurchases cannot assure a price floor. ASM · 11–16 Nov · CONFIRMED FACT

E. Opportunities and durable competitive position

  • Leading-edge atomic layer deposition: more precisely controlled layers at 2nm and 1.4nm make process complexity a long-lived tool driver; issuer expects served market growth at 1.4nm.
  • Memory and HBM: added processing steps and DRAM investment lifted Q3 memory-equipment sales, but end-market demand does not guarantee every fab tools order.
  • Spares and services: Q3 service/supply revenue €223.1m, +32% reported YoY, diversifying some of the tool cycle.
  • Cash returns: €300m buyback initiated 11 November, with 2.2% executed by 13 November; it is a small percentage of a ~€33bn company and not an unconditional support mechanism.

Monitor shipments, gross-margin trajectory around 52%, capex and customer/fab expansion, supply lead times, export-policy developments and any 2027 guidance revision. ASM results · ASM capital returns
