Advanced logic/foundry: 1.4nm and 2nm manufacturing require more ALD deposition steps; ASM expects more market opportunity and stronger share at newer nodes.

AI memory: DRAM/HBM manufacturing upgrades create equipment demand, particularly high-spec ALD and epitaxy, although fab timing can shift.

Services and innovation: equipment spares/services grew 32% reported YoY in Q3; flowable carbon, epitaxy and advanced packaging technologies offer additional process coverage. The company expects 2027 revenue >€5.7B, targeting levels initially anticipated for 2030.
