A. Why has the stock weakened despite sales growth?

Why the price moved

Sales acceleration is real, but reported EPS and the cost of acquisitions complicate Ecolab's near-term earnings valuation. No single trigger for the latest selloff is proven.

▤
Strong reported sales have not translated into GAAP EPS gains.

The 27 October report shows Q3 sales $4.695bn (+13%) and organic sales +7%, but reported diluted EPS $1.95 versus $2.05 a year earlier (−5%). Adjusted EPS rose 5% to $2.18. Higher charges and acquisition costs partly explain the gap, which could depress valuation even with healthy sales; this is not demonstrated as the cause of a particular session's fall. SEC Q3 release · Oct 27 CONFIRMED FACT; price interpretation plausible

◇
The CoolIT acquisition raised debt and financial sensitivity.

Ecolab closed the CoolIT purchase on 2 July, paying roughly $4.63bn net. At September 30, net debt/adjusted EBITDA was 2.9× versus 1.6× a year earlier. Higher interest and integration demands can reduce how much shareholders benefit immediately from high-tech growth, even while long-term opportunities improve. Q3 issuer slides · Oct 27 CONFIRMED FACT; price interpretation plausible

⌁
AI cooling announcements offer meaningful upside counterevidence.

Ecolab introduced an integrated cooling platform 16 November and new ecosystem partnerships 17 November. Its Global High-Tech operation was described as approaching $1.5bn annualized 2026 sales including purchases, with a $4bn 2030 aspiration. Forecast adoption and economics remain unverified, so the announcement itself is not proof of future stock returns. Issuer announcement · Nov 16 CONFIRMED ANNOUNCEMENT; management aspiration

↘
The share pullback coincided with wider materials weakness.

From the completed 13 November to 19 November IEX sessions ECL returned -3.79%, while XLB returned -5.32%. ECL outperformed by +1.52 percentage points despite falling. This supports a broad-market contribution hypothesis, not a causal explanation; relative prices alone cannot attribute an individual session.
