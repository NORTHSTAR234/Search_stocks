E. Growth opportunities

  1. Accelerated AI training and inference: Data Center demand can expand with higher model inference workloads and increasing GPU compute use.
  2. Vera Rubin, networking and complete systems: full-stack integration and network fabric may increase economic share per deployed AI factory; integration and HBM supply constrain the upside.
  3. Enterprise, sovereign AI and physical AI: broader customer adoption could diversify hyperscaler concentration, but addressable-market narratives are not contracted revenues.

CUDA's software ecosystem and deployment complexity provide switching friction, while rival accelerators and custom ASICs impose ongoing pricing pressure.
