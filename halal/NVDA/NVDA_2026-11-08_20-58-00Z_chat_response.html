E. Main growth opportunities

NVIDIA's strongest growth opportunities are large-scale AI training and inference, its Vera Rubin platform, advanced networking, enterprise AI and physical AI applications. Its software ecosystem could reinforce customer switching costs, while broader AI adoption could support demand beyond the largest hyperscalers.

The central question is whether customers can generate enough returns from AI infrastructure to sustain the extraordinary spending required by these forecasts.

F. Risks and the next major catalyst

▣ Confirmed earnings: Tuesday, 17 November 2026

NVIDIA expects to announce Q3 results at approximately 1:20 PM Pacific, with the earnings call at 2 PM Pacific. In Melbourne, that corresponds to approximately 8:20 AM and 9:00 AM on Wednesday, 18 November.

The most important risks are concentrated hyperscaler capital spending, custom-chip competition, restrictions on China sales, shortages and increasing costs of memory, and pressure on gross margins. These risks are material even if wider geopolitical tensions ease.

The company's Q2 earnings commentary projected higher memory costs and possible Q4 gross margins of 71–72%, lower than Q2's 75%.
