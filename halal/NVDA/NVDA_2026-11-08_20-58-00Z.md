## E. Business growth opportunities

1. **Data Center, inference and hyperscalers:** accelerators, compute/networking fabric, CUDA ecosystem. Company reported FY2027 Q2 Data Center revenue $89B and Vera Rubin in production. Strong custom architecture and software integration support switching costs.
2. **Vera Rubin and system-level hardware:** performance efficiency and system/networking content could drive increased revenue per installed AI system. Memory scarcity and integration remain constraints.
3. **AI-native startups, enterprise, sovereign AI and physical AI:** diversify customer demand; monetisation must justify large capex and NVIDIA share of budgets.
4. **Gaming, professional visualisation, automotive/robotics:** diversified platforms but currently materially smaller than Data Center in revenue contributions. Do not confuse narrative TAMs with booked revenue.
