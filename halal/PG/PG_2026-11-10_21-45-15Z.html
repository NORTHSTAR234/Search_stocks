FY26 reference: $87.0 billion net sales (+3%), $6.62 GAAP EPS (+2%), $6.89 core EPS (+1%). FY27 guidance: organic sales growth 1–3%, core EPS $6.89–7.11 (midpoint $7.00), or 0–3% core EPS growth. Updated all-in sales growth guidance 2–4% includes the Thorne acquisition.

Valuation: current $168.63 / management FY27 core EPS midpoint $7.00 = approximately 24.1× forward management-guided adjusted earnings. This is a management-guidance-based P/E, not consensus. The forward premium is much harder to justify when profit growth guidance is only 0–3%. For approximate context, same-period market-data snapshots show peer CL at roughly 24–26× and KMB at roughly 16× forward earnings; peer forecasts and reporting years differ and are not directly apples-to-apples.

Strengths: high brand visibility and repeat purchases, worldwide distribution, ongoing productivity investment. Weaknesses: slow current EPS expansion and price sensitivity to even small earnings disappointments.

E. Growth opportunities

  • Premium innovation: trading shoppers up to stronger-performing premium products can support mix and unit economics, provided price-sensitive households accept premiums.
  • Productivity and portfolio reset: savings and selective brand/product discontinuations could finance reinvestment and eventually lift margins; P&G expects $1–1.6B of restructuring costs over its two-year programme.
  • Distribution expansion and Thorne: international penetration, digital channels and acquisition-led health/wellness diversification may broaden revenue, but Thorne's incremental revenue is not equivalent to organic growth.

F. Risks and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS: 20 January 2027 — ESTIMATED, NOT COMPANY-CONFIRMED. External estimates for fiscal Q2 core EPS are about $1.81–1.82; analyst estimates are subject to revision.

Confirmed near-term event: P&G Investor Day on 19 November 2026. Focus on organic volume trajectory, productivity and reinvestment, core operating margin, cost inflation and any updated FY27 outlook. Core Q1 operating margin declined 80 bps, an important negative offset to sales growth.

  • Consumer trade-down: private labels and competitors can constrain pricing and volume.
  • Cost/FX/geopolitics: transport, inputs, tariffs and currency moves can offset savings.
  • Valuation compression: an approximately 24× guided core EPS multiple leaves limited margin for weak growth.

Fundamental invalidation would be sustained organic-volume deterioration, repeated guidance reductions or material cash-conversion weakness. A short-term technical stop is not itself evidence of business failure.
