A · Investment case and price movement

Why the price moved

A. Why is JNJ falling even as reported sales rise?

Revenue growth is real, but acquisition costs, lower adjusted earnings and legal exposure make investors less certain about the profits behind those sales. The exact cause of the 18 November share-price fall is not established.

▤
Higher revenue has not translated into higher adjusted EPS.

J&J reported Q3 sales of $25.399 billion, up 5.9% from a year earlier, while adjusted EPS declined 7.5% to $2.59. It raised the 2026 sales and EPS outlook, but acquisition-related costs reduce near-term earnings power; a causal link to the 18 November decline is unproven. J&J · 13 Oct 2026 CONFIRMED FACT · PRICE EFFECT PLAUSIBLE

⚖
Talc litigation remains a meaningful cash risk.

Reuters reported on 17 November that trial lawyer Mark Lanier joined a long-running shareholder lawsuit concerning J&J talc disclosures. A lawsuit is not a finding of liability, and no verified evidence isolates it as the reason for the latest price move; it does add uncertainty around future legal costs. ◇
New ophthalmology growth comes with upfront spending.

On 16 November J&J announced an agreement to acquire Ollin Biosciences for $2.2 billion upfront, with possible additional milestone payments. Its late-stage retinal pipeline could help long-run growth, but acquisition funding and clinical uncertainty may constrain near-term returns.
