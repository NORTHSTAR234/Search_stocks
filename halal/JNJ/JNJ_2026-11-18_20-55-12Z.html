Why the price moved

A. Why is JNJ falling despite higher sales and new drug opportunities?

Sales grew in the latest reported quarter, but acquisition costs, lower adjusted earnings and unresolved talc risks may limit what investors are willing to pay. The precise cause of the Nov 16–18 decline is not established.

◫
Sales growth is not translating into stronger earnings.

JNJ reported Q3 sales of $25.399 billion, up 5.9%, but adjusted EPS fell 7.5% to $2.59. Its $0.09 GAAP EPS reflects large special charges; this helps explain why a revenue beat may not persuade valuation-focused holders. J&J · 13 Oct 2026 CONFIRMED RESULTS; PRICE EFFECT PLAUSIBLE

⚖
Talc litigation adds uncertainty to future cash needs.

Reuters reported on 17 November that a prominent trial lawyer joined an existing shareholder suit concerning historic talc disclosures. This is a litigation development, not a judgment against JNJ; no source establishes that it caused the latest price decline. Reuters · 17 Nov 2026 REPORTED EVENT; CAUSATION UNPROVEN

◇
An ophthalmology acquisition adds upside and cash commitments.

On 16 November JNJ announced a $2.2 billion upfront plan to buy Ollin Biosciences, bringing an investigational Phase 3 retinal therapy. A successful product could enlarge the pipeline, but the trial and approval are not assured, and the upfront expense can weigh on cash. J&J · 16 Nov 2026 CONFIRMED ANNOUNCEMENT; MIXED IMPLICATIONS
