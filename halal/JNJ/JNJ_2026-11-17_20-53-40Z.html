1. Johnson & Johnson investment analysis

Why the price moved

A. Why is JNJ falling while sales are growing?

Sales have risen, but profitability, costly investments and legal uncertainty leave less room for earnings disappointments at a premium valuation. Today's intraday selloff has no demonstrated single cause.

▤
Higher sales are not delivering equally strong earnings.

Johnson & Johnson reported third-quarter sales of $25.399bn, up 5.9%, while adjusted EPS fell 7.5% to $2.59. Litigation and acquisition costs also pushed GAAP EPS to $0.09, creating uncertainty about how much growth converts into shareholder profit. J&amp;J Q3 · 13 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED ANNOUNCEMENT / PLAUSIBLE PRICE CONTRIBUTOR

⚖
A new shareholder lawsuit development adds uncertainty.

Reuters reported on 17 November that a prominent lawyer joined a longstanding investor class action concerning historical talc disclosures. This is a reported legal development, not a ruling against J&J, and there is no reliable evidence isolating it as the cause of the same-day share decline. Reuters · 17 Nov 2026 · REPORTED LITIGATION DEVELOPMENT

◈
The Ollin deal strengthens the pipeline but adds spending.

On 16 November J&J disclosed a $2.2bn upfront acquisition of Ollin, with up to $1.125bn of later milestones and separate Blackstone development funding. The Phase 3 retinal treatment could add growth, but it is not approved and financial benefits are uncertain; the announcement does not prove why the share price moved.
