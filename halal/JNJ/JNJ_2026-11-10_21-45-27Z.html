E. Growth opportunities

  1. Oncology and immunology: DARZALEX, CARVYKTI and TREMFYA, plus drug launches, can offset patent-driven erosion if patient access and uptake support higher-value sales.
  2. MedTech robotics and cardiovascular: OTTAVA surgery and Shockwave cardiovascular products could improve procedure and device revenue, subject to execution and hospital budgets.
  3. Pipeline and new indications: 2026 clinical/approval progress such as IMAAVY and RYBREVANT FASPRO regulatory work can create multiyear commercial opportunities; trial success alone does not ensure monetization.

F. Risks and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS: TUESDAY 26 JANUARY 2027 — ISSUER-ANNOUNCED DATE. JNJ cited this fourth-quarter reporting date in its Q3 results call announcement. Monitor Q4 results, adjusted FY2027 outlook, segment growth, organic vs acquired growth, EPS revisions and free cash flow. Watch Nov 24 ex-dividend.

  1. Talc litigation: judgments, settlements, delays and legal cash requirements may pressure headline GAAP earnings and valuation.
  2. Pharma competition: patent expirations, biosimilars, reimbursement and drug pricing could undermine branded-medicine cash flows.
  3. Execution and multiple compression: clinical, acquired-asset and robotics commercialization disappointments could be magnified by a ~24× FY2027 multiple.

Thesis invalidation: sustained core Innovative Medicine decline, materially larger unprovided-for legal cash obligations, or a substantive reset lower in earnings/FCF guidance. Technical levels do not supersede fundamental review.
