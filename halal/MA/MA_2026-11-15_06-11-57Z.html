E. Growth opportunities

  • More electronic payments and cross-border transaction volume expand switched and value-added revenues; higher cross-border fees can support margins but introduce cyclicality.
  • Services, identity, cyber/fraud, open banking and data tools diversify beyond core card processing.
  • Management's announced BVNK Global Money Platform collaboration and agentic commerce initiatives create optionality, but contribution to near-term 2027 EPS has not been separately proven.
  • Share repurchases can increase per-share growth, subject to purchase price and cash allocation.
