E. Growth thesis and catalysts

  • Payments network: ongoing digitization, card and commercial payment penetration and transaction switching.
  • Cross-border: travel and international spending volume growth; sensitive to macro slowdowns and FX effects.
  • Services: +19% Q3 revenue expansion in fraud, security, data and related offerings helps diversify away from transaction switching.
  • Innovation: open banking, Global Money Platform/BVNK, agentic commerce and tokenized payment use cases can extend the addressable market, but growth/return timing remains uncertain.
