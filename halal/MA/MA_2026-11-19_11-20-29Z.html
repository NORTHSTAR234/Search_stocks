A. Why has Mastercard pulled back after strong earnings?

Mastercard's earnings accelerated, yet the shares retreated in mid-November as broad-market weakness and unresolved payment-fee litigation kept risk in focus; individual daily causes cannot be proved from correlation.

On 29 October Mastercard reported Q3 net revenue of $9.807bn, 14% above a year earlier, and adjusted EPS of $5.31, about 21% higher.
