E. Durable competitive strengths and growth opportunities

Mastercard's global acceptance network benefits from issuer, acquirer and merchant integration, fraud tools and valuable data. Cross-border spending and switched volumes generate scalable network economics. Value-added services, cybersecurity, open banking and the new Mastercard Accept product can broaden revenue sources beyond transaction switching. Management is also investing in digital assets and agentic commerce. New products' future EPS impact has not been quantified in audited financial results and must be treated as optionality rather than certain profit.

Payment-network alternatives, pricing incentives, consumers' real disposable income and regulatory changes can temper growth. Stock repurchases increase per-share EPS but may destroy value if done at excessive prices. Mastercard Accept release
