A. Why did Sandisk fall despite its record earnings?

Why the price moved

Record NAND profits are real, but investors are increasingly worried they represent the top of a volatile memory-pricing cycle rather than sustainable long-term earnings.

📉
The memory-pricing outlook weakened sharply.

TrendForce's 19 Nov research warned of a NAND supply-demand reversal in 2027, forecasting contract-price declines of 0–5% quarter-on-quarter in Q1 and broader weakness beginning Q2. Pricing has driven much of Sandisk's strong profitability, so a reversal could reduce future margins rapidly. CONFIRMED INDUSTRY FORECAST; PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR · TrendForce, 19 Nov

💾
Unusually high NAND margins may not endure.

Sandisk's 29 Oct filing showed Q1 FY2027 revenue US$11.03bn, 85.0% GAAP gross margin and net income US$8.10bn. A price-sensitive business can be highly profitable at the cycle peak, yet trade down on anticipation of lower next-year selling prices. The company, meanwhile, guided Q2 FY2027 revenue to US$12.2–12.6bn. CONFIRMED FINANCIAL FACT; PRICE EFFECT INFERRED · Company Q1 results, 29 Oct

📊
Technology stocks declined as investors reduced risk.

On 20 Nov the Nasdaq Composite fell about 2.29% according to its index operator, while SNDK declined 15.0% on the same completed regular session. That is about 12.7 percentage points of relative underperformance, consistent with company/industry-specific repricing on top of broad tech pressure; correlation does not prove exact trading motives. E. Competitive position and growth drivers

Sandisk designs NAND flash, enterprise SSD and edge/consumer storage. The company's datacenter revenue reached US$4.802B in Q1 FY27, up 61% sequentially, while Edge brought US$5.832B and consumer revenue US$0.397B declined 29% quarter-on-quarter. This mix supports AI storage demand, yet makes future datacenter demand and NAND wafer pricing unusually important.

Its New Business Model customer agreements underpin an SEC-disclosed US$88.9B remaining performance-obligation amount at 2 Oct 2026, of which approximately 20% is expected to convert to revenue over the following twelve months. This is not free cash, does not guarantee future margins, and remaining obligations carry execution/contract risks. Sandisk's Kioxia relationship and product road map also matter. Issuer 10-Q contractual obligations.
