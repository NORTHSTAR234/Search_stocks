U.S. technology stocks fell sharply on 19–20 November, and Nasdaq-100 exposure weakened alongside concerns about the outlook for rates and high-growth valuations. Management guided to at least $755 million Q4 revenue and at least $280 million adjusted EBITDA. Both figures create measurable next-report hurdles, but they are forecasts rather than realized sales or cash, and a previous post-earnings rally has now reversed. Issuer Q4 guidance · 9 Nov 2026 CONFIRMED FACT
