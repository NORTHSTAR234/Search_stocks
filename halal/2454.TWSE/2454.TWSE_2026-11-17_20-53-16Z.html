E. Growth opportunities

MediaTek is expanding from mobile and connected devices into custom AI data-center accelerators. Management says September 2026 initial shipments began, forecasts roughly US$2.5bn of Q4 accelerator revenue and aims for approximately US$16bn from the programme in 2027, while a second project targets first-quarter 2028 volume production. These are issuer forecasts with sizable delivery, customer and supply dependencies. 30 Oct issuer call.

The Smart Edge Platforms group also benefited from AI PCs/tablets, 5G modems, automotive cockpit projects and device computing; broadening end markets reduces sole reliance on smartphone chips, but does not guarantee attractive margins. Management's Q4 gross-margin guide is 45% ±1.5 pp; the forecasted higher ASIC mix may dilute gross margin even as operating margin improves. Issuer segment commentary · Product news.
