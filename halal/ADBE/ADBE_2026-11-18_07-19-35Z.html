1. Adobe investment analysis

Why the price moved

A. Why is Adobe falling while revenue still grows?

Investors are marking down the price they are willing to pay for Adobe's current profits because they fear AI competitors could change its future economics, even though reported sales have continued to rise.

17 Nov matched market comparison: ADBE −4.25% ($246.00→$235.55), S&P 500 −1.03%, underperformance −3.22 percentage points. This comparison describes correlation, not proven causation. Sep/Q3 fundamentals and subsequent company news do not establish an intraday cause by themselves.

Citi sees a hard reset in future growth

Because the rating change predates 17 Nov, it may be a lingering factor rather than that day's trigger.

Strong sales do not eliminate AI monetisation risk

Adobe reported Q3 revenue of $6.76bn, up 13%, but Q4 revenue guidance of $6.80–6.85bn had a $6.825bn midpoint marginally below the $6.85bn analyst estimate reported at the time. Customers may adopt Firefly and other AI features without raising spend enough to justify historic software multiples; that is a risk hypothesis, not an observed subscription collapse.
