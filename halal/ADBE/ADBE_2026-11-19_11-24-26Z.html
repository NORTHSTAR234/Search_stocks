That gap can make future incremental profit growth less reassuring than headline sales imply. It is a company-confirmed operating observation, not evidence that it directly caused November's decline. It is positive counterevidence, not proof that Adobe's competitive risk has disappeared. Adobe newsroom · 10 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED ANNOUNCEMENT / UNVERIFIED ECONOMICS
