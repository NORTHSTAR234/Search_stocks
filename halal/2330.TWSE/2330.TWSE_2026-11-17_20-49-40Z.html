E. Growth opportunities

1. Further 2nm adoption. Q3 2nm revenue already made up 8% of wafer sales; node migration can raise manufacturing value, subject to customer yields and depreciation. TSMC Q3.

2. AI/HPC demand. Major accelerators use advanced processes and packaging, so AI data-centre orders could support strong utilisation; Nvidia's imminent results are an informative customer indicator, not a TSMC purchase-order disclosure. Nvidia events.

3. Geographic expansion. New manufacturing capacity may diversify supply and win strategic customers, but fab power, labor and depreciation costs could compress free cash flow if demand is slower than expected. Cash flow and capex.
