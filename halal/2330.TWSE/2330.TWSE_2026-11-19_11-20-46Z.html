Why the price moved

A. Why is TSMC falling despite strong chip sales?

Revenue and net profit are rising sharply, but valuation, market sentiment and US chip-policy uncertainty can push shares down before company earnings visibly weaken.

↘
Taiwan equities sold off sharply on Thursday.

On 19 November, TSMC closed at NT$2,100, down 3.45%, while the TAIEX lost 4.40% in the same regular session. Widespread risk reduction offers a plausible setting, but simultaneous losses do not identify which investors sold TSMC or why. TWSE · 19 Nov · TAIEX · 19 Nov · CONFIRMED MARKET FACT; CAUSE PLAUSIBLE

⇄
Fresh export controls add uncertainty to chip demand.

Reuters reported on 17 November that US advanced-AI-chip restrictions coincided with falling TSMC and Samsung shares; a 19 November Financial Times report discussed affected shipments to some Chinese customers. Specific order losses or material earnings reductions for TSMC are not quantified in the sourced issuer results, so the price impact remains a plausible inference. Reuters · 17 Nov · FT · 19 Nov · REPORTED FACT; CAUSAL EFFECT UNVERIFIED

▣
Profits are growing faster than sales.

TSMC reported Q3 revenue of NT$1,494.24bn (+50.9% YoY) and net profit of NT$763.06bn (+68.7% YoY), while October revenue rose 59.7% YoY. Those strong results argue against assuming deteriorating core demand from price action alone; highly valued growth shares can nonetheless fall when future expectations reprice. TSMC SEC · 15 Oct · TSMC · 10 Nov · CONFIRMED FUNDAMENTALS; REPRICING PLAUSIBLE

▤
Aggressive capacity investment could pressure future returns.

TSMC's 10 November board approved around US$40.93bn of capital appropriations, which is approval rather than actual cash already spent. Expansion may preserve leading-edge advantage, but investors must assess utilisation, depreciation and margins if chip demand slows. Q3 operating cash flow still exceeded Q3 actual capex.
