E. Growth opportunities

  1. AI and HPC leading nodes: Advanced GPU and custom accelerators require more leading-edge silicon; greater 2nm/3nm utilization can sustain price and mix advantages. Q3 advanced nodes were 79% of wafer revenue.
  2. Advanced packaging and chip stacking: CoWoS/3DFabric-type capacity can benefit from chiplet architectures and increase content per AI system, conditional on execution and customer demand.
  3. Overseas and specialized fabs: Arizona, Japan and European footprints diversify customer supply resilience and extend process participation, though cost structures may dilute margins relative to Taiwan fabs.
