A 7 Nov Reuters story reported possible China-operation carve-out alternatives, but these are unconfirmed media reports, not a signed solution. ABB official update; Reuters coverage.

  1. Transaction failure / reduced cash proceeds: altered terms, regulatory delay or cancellation could compress valuation and change shareholder returns.
  2. Order-cycle reversal: +40% comparable order growth is exceptional and should not be assumed recurring. Watch AI/data-center, grid and industrial growth, backlog conversion and the 2027 outlook.
  3. Multiple / CHF / tariffs / macro: global demand, currency translation, rates and M&A adjustment risks affect profits and share price.

ABB Q3 issuer guidance for FY2026 was low-teens comparable revenue growth and ≥270 basis points improvement in reported operational EBITA margin; Q4 margin expected to sequentially soften versus Q3, excluding the cited property-gain effect. Main invalidation: sustained weakness in core orders or free cash flow together with serious Robotics deal impairment, not simply a technical stop breach.
