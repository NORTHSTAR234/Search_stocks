E. Growth opportunities

  • Data centres and AI power infrastructure: ABB can supply power distribution, switchgear, circuit protection and associated services for high-density loads; potential growth subject to backlog conversion and project execution.
  • Grid modernisation and electrification: utility renewals and industrial electrification create repeat demand for energy management, automation and service revenues.
  • Portfolio focus and partnerships: a successful Robotics divestiture could streamline capital allocation; separately, the Mitsubishi Electric low-voltage partnership offers potential growth but needs transaction execution and is not automatic accretion.

Three event reactions: (a) credible fully agreed transaction preserving value: potential upgrade of WATCH upon price and deal confirmation; (b) protracted carve-out without numbers: retain WATCH/HOLD and avoid chasing; (c) sale failure with weaker order outlook: reassess risk and possibly REDUCE or SELL on thesis break.
