  • Grid modernization/electrification: utilities and industrial decarbonization invest in electrical resilience and efficient motion; ABB's installed base can support service, upgrades and high-value automation.
  • Portfolio optimization and integration: successful Robotics separation and Rotork transaction could sharpen margins and the capital allocation thesis, but approvals, valuations and integration timing are uncertainties, not guaranteed catalysts.

F. Risks and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS — 28 JANUARY 2027 · ISSUER-CONFIRMED
Earlier catalyst: ABB Group Capital Markets Day, 18 November 2026 · issuer calendar.

Key current development (5 Nov 2026): China's foreign-investment review authority has prohibited the acquisition of ABB's China Robotics business under ABB's US$5.375B Robotics sale agreement with SoftBank. The parties remain in dialogue. Do not interpret this as a fully terminated global deal; similarly do not assume resolution.

  • Deal execution: inability to close as planned could disrupt cash proceeds, valuation and shareholder return expectations. Monitor precise revised terms.
  • Order-cycle reversal: Q3 40% comparable orders is exceptional; easing AI/data-center, industrial or utility investment could slow future revenue/operating leverage.
  • Valuation/FX/macro: a ~19.5x forward multiple remains sensitive to rate assumptions, tariff changes, CHF strength, M&A charges and foreign-exchange translation.

Thesis watch points: China/SoftBank resolution, order-to-revenue conversion, EBITA margin durability relative to the company's FY2026 guidance, FCF quality and any FY2027 targets issued on 18 Nov. FY2026 company guidance in Q3 called for low-teens comparable revenue growth and ≥270bps annual operational EBITA margin improvement. Monitor rather than extrapolate Q3 directly.
