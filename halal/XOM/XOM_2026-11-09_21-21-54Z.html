E. Growth opportunities

  • Guyana and low-cost upstream: production scale and efficient reservoirs support cash generation at varying commodity prices.
  • Permian, Kearl and global LNG: project optimisation and ramp-ups diversify long-run volumes.
  • Refining and integration: strong utilization and product mix can capture favorable margins, though gains reverse in weak cycles.
  • Capital returns: management described $20B of 2027 share repurchases, subject to conditions; cost savings support resilience.
  • Lower-emissions businesses: carbon capture, fuels and related projects offer options, with uncertain timelines and returns.

F. Risks and upcoming events

Next results: Q4 2026, estimated around 29 January 2027; not yet confirmed by the company. Finviz displays an illustrative adjusted EPS estimate of $4.08 for that quarter, dependent on commodity conditions. Near-term ex-dividend: 16 November 2026, independently listed by dividend vendor.
  • Oil and natural-gas prices can fall rapidly; a 9 November crude selloff illustrated ongoing volatility.
  • Refining margins, geopolitics, shipping availability and OPEC+ policy materially affect earnings.
  • Execution risk in Guyana, LNG expansions and capital-intensive upstream developments.
  • Carbon/methane regulation, environmental liabilities, reserve replacement and transition policy.
  • Short-term technical weakness: IEX closes below 20- and 50-day averages; large share-price moves and gap risk.
  • USD/AUD exposure, US withholding tax and Australian investor-specific tax may affect realised returns.

Fundamental thesis invalidation: persistent deterioration in cash-generation through the commodity cycle, excessive capex or leverage, material project failures, or unsustainable FCF dividend coverage. Price-only stops do not replace analysis of those factors.
