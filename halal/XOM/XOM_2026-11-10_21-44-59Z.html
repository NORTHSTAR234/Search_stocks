E. Growth opportunities

  1. Guyana and Permian: Scale and relatively advantaged resources can improve output and unit economics when projects execute well.
  2. LNG expansion: Greater involvement in Papua and Mozambique LNG offers long-term growth and geographic diversification, subject to project and geopolitical execution risk.
  3. Refining and cost improvements: Strong fuels utilization and diesel yields can support margins and increase resilience alongside structural efficiencies.

These are opportunities identified from issuer operations, not earnings forecasts with guaranteed precision.
