E. Growth opportunities

  • U.S. upstream productivity: production up 27% over two years; continued output efficiency can strengthen cash flow across crude cycles.
  • Venezuela and other international assets: enhanced acreage supports longer-run oil production, but creates sanctions and geopolitics exposure.
  • Refinery/throughput economics: record U.S. refinery crude throughput and 98% utilization in Q3 offer downstream diversification, though margins are cyclical.
