E. Growth opportunities

  1. Hess/Guyana and Permian: large-resource production and efficiency investments could improve per-unit costs and reserves productivity.
  2. Venezuela, LNG and international exploration: expanded Venezuelan acreage, Angola discovery and Libya exploration create resource optionality, subject to sanctions, execution and capital needs.
  3. Downstream and Bakken savings: record U.S. refinery throughput at 98% utilisation and planned Bakken midstream-cost reductions create operating leverage while spreads remain favourable.
