E. Growth opportunities

  1. Hess/Guyana and Permian scale: efficient large-resource growth can support volume expansion and unit-cost improvements if project delivery remains strong.
  2. Venezuela and LNG: Chevron's September 2026 agreements envisage expanded Venezuelan acreage, more than $7B of potential spending over five years and ambitions to increase production, subject to country and political risks. LNG diversification may help balance the portfolio.
  3. Refining performance and operational optimization: third-quarter US refinery crude use reached 98% and company-wide throughput records support downstream profitability; refining spreads remain cyclical.

These are conditional opportunities, not booked future profits; high capital intensity and geopolitical constraints must be assessed alongside production potential.
