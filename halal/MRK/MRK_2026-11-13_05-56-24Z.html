E. Growth opportunities

  1. WINREVAIR: Q3 pulmonary arterial hypertension product revenue $694M, +93%, could partly diversify the earnings mix if patient access and treatment penetration grow.
  2. Oncology: KEYTRUDA QLEX, sac-TMT antibody–drug conjugate and a therapeutic cancer vaccine have clinical/approval potential but will need execution and substantial sales to cushion future KEYTRUDA exclusivity losses.
  3. Efinopegdutide and animal health: positive 9 Nov Phase 2b MASH data provide new optionality, but not product revenue today; animal health Q3 sales ~$1.7B (+4% YoY) diversify earnings.

F. Risks, news and upcoming events

NEXT EARNINGS: 2 FEBRUARY 2027 · 9:00 AM ET · ISSUER CONFIRMED.

Closer: 19 November 2026, Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference in London (official Merck event). Review comments on 2027 guidance, KEYTRUDA franchises, WINREVAIR demand and acquisition integration; the next quarterly earnings release is more than 30 days away.

  1. KEYTRUDA patent cliff: loss-of-exclusivity challenges around 2028 and beyond could suppress price/volume; new oncology products must grow sufficiently fast to offset erosion.
  2. R&D and acquisition delivery: expensive Cidara/Terns deal charges and other portfolio partnerships increase execution and balance-sheet risk; trials and FDA submissions can fail.
  3. Pricing, reimbursement and pipeline dependence: pressure on US and overseas drug prices, safety findings, competition and insurer access can upset expected earnings.

Major thesis-invalidation signals: sustained falling 2027/2028 earnings consensus, weakening recurring cash flow, worsened debt load and evidence that replacement products cannot offset KEYTRUDA erosion. On 9 Nov MASH results were scientifically positive, but market-wide rebound was larger than MRK's price change.
