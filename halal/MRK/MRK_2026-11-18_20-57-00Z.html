Why the price moved

A. Why did Merck fall sharply after its clinical setback?

Investors were already assessing future Keytruda replacement growth; an unexpected discontinued trial programme has added pressure even though the existing medicine franchise continues generating cash.

⚕
The BELLWAVE blood-cancer programme was stopped.

After market close on 17 Nov 2026, Merck discontinued nemtabrutinib development after the Phase 3 BELLWAVE-011 external monitoring committee's benefit-risk review. This removes a potential development asset from future expectations; it is a plausible contributor to the following day's weakness, not proof it alone caused every dollar of decline. Merck · 17 Nov · Reuters coverage CONFIRMED FACT / price causation inferred

▤
Sales growth is outpacing reported profit growth.

Merck's Q3 2026 sales rose 5% to $18.085bn, but adjusted EPS of $2.49 was 3% lower than a year earlier. Growth in Keytruda/Qlex and Winrevair is encouraging, yet net profit conversion and acquisition-related charges remain relevant to how investors value future earnings. The quarterly report predates this particular selloff. Merck Q3 · 29 Oct CONFIRMED BACKGROUND

⌛
Keytruda's U.S. patent transition remains a major concern.

Keytruda/Qlex accounted for $8.6bn of third-quarter sales. Merck's own patent disclosures list 2028 as the main U.S. Keytruda expiry year, so new products need to offset eventual erosion; this is a structural valuation overhang, not a new 18 November filing. Merck 2025 Form 10-K patent table CONFIRMED LONG-TERM RISK

✚
Other clinical and commercial franchises remain constructive.

Q3 Keytruda/Qlex sales advanced 6% and Winrevair sales rose 93% to $694m; Merck also reported encouraging oncology and cardiopulmonary pipeline milestones. These are credible positive counterweights, but do not erase the 17 November discontinued trial or guarantee quick share-price recovery. Merck Q3 · 29 Oct CONFIRMED POSITIVE COUNTEREVIDENCE
