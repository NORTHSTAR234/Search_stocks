E. Growth opportunities

  • WINREVAIR for pulmonary arterial hypertension grew 93% year over year in Q3 to $694M, supporting non-Keytruda growth.
  • Oncology programs: sac-TMT and cancer-vaccine collaboration trial progress reported at ESMO; commercial and approval outcomes remain uncertain.
  • Metabolic/liver portfolio: Nov 9 efinopegdutide Phase 2b MASH study positive, but no final approval or 2027 commercial sales should be assumed.
  • Europe: Two Nov 13 CHMP opinions provide potential future WELIREG and ENFLONSIA access expansions, not yet marketing authorizations.

F. Risks and upcoming events

Main risks: Keytruda patent loss-of-exclusivity (compound patent expires Dec 2028, with potential later May/Nov 2029 method patent protection subject to defense), drug pricing pressures, vaccine sales variability, clinical setbacks, takeover/pipeline execution, debt burden and already strong prior price gains. Past phase-trial success does not ensure commercialization.
