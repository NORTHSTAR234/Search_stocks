A · Why Merck shares fell

Why the price moved

A. Why did the stock fall even as revenue grew?

Investors face improving current sales but uncertainty about future products: a late-stage clinical program was halted just before a pronounced share-price decline.

⚕
The BELLWAVE blood-cancer program was terminated.

On 17 November at 4:15 p.m. EST, Merck announced that it would discontinue all nemtabrutinib development after a Phase 3 benefit–risk review. This removed a potential future oncology asset and plausibly contributed to the following session's decline, although no news event can explain every trade. Issuer · 17 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED FACT · CAUSAL LINK PLAUSIBLE

▤
Sales growth has not become comparable earnings growth.

Third-quarter sales rose 5% to $18.085 billion, while adjusted EPS fell 3% to $2.49. Product mix, business development and licensing charges help explain why the headline sales improvement need not support a higher earnings multiple; these results were known since 29 October. Q3 issuer results · 29 Oct · CONFIRMED BACKGROUND

⌛
Keytruda patent exposure raises the stakes for replacements.

Keytruda and Qlex generated $8.6 billion in third-quarter revenue, and the key United States exclusivity period begins to change in 2028. Losing a late-stage candidate is consequential to long-term replacement expectations, but this patent issue predates the 18 November selloff. Issuer 2026 10-Q · CONFIRMED LONG-TERM RISK · NOT NEW CAUSATION

✚
Other fast-growing medicines remain a positive counterweight.

Merck reported 6% Keytruda/Qlex quarterly growth and Winrevair sales of $694 million, up 93%. Existing franchises and strong cash generation could help Merck absorb individual development failures; they are not a guarantee of stock-price recovery. Merck · 29 Oct · CONFIRMED POSITIVE EVIDENCE
