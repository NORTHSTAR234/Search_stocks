E. Growth opportunities and business model

  • Ethernet: controllers, transceivers, switch chips and multi-gigabit/10G networking; management expects speed and product-specification upgrades to support average selling price and mix.
  • Wi-Fi 7 and next wireless generations: routers, client solutions and smart-connected products. Product ramps depend on OEM design wins and adoption timelines.
  • 10G PON/broadband and edge connectivity: potential benefit from network upgrades, home and enterprise demand; not identical to direct AI accelerator exposure.
  • Automotive Ethernet/AIoT: higher-value connectivity content may diversify a legacy PC/peripheral exposure.

Management's Q3 discussion pointed to stable Q4 orders and steady 2027 sales growth but cited weaker PCs and foundry/OSAT cost pressure. Strong 2026 top-line growth supports the opportunity; future gross margin recovery is still a thesis, not a result.

Summary & evaluation

Long-duration product-mix potential constructive; realisation depends on cost control and pricing.
