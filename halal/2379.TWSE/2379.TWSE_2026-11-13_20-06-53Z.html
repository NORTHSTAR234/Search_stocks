E. Growth opportunities

  1. Wi-Fi 7 / Wi-Fi 8: wider Wi-Fi 7 penetration and expected Wi-Fi 8 product ramps can lift average selling prices and upgrade content per device; timing and uptake uncertain.
  2. Multi-gigabit Ethernet / managed switches / 10G PON: management disclosed multi-gigabit Ethernet was more than 40% of Ethernet revenue in Q3. Higher specifications can improve mix and scale but require competitive pricing.
  3. Automotive Ethernet, AI/regular servers, edge AI and AIoT: incremental design wins create end-market diversification; premium connectivity is not the same as guaranteed direct AI accelerator demand.

Management on 29 Oct projected steady 2027 top-line growth, offset by higher semiconductor supply-chain costs and weak traditional PC demand.
