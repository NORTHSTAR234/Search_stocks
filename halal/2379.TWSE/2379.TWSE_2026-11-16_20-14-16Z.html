Why the price moved

A. Why is Realtek falling when sales are growing?

Revenue grew in 2026, but profit conversion lagged while Taiwan semiconductor shares faced a market-wide selloff. The exact cause of Realtek's 16 November change is not established.

▥
Taiwan's technology market faced intense selling pressure.

The Taiwan Weighted index fell 6.03% on 16 November against Realtek's 5.54% fall. This is contemporaneous sector context, not proof of why this precise security traded down. TWSE · 16 Nov PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

◫
Higher revenue is generating little additional net profit.

Realtek's first nine months of 2026 produced NT$111.71bn in revenue, +15.85% YoY, yet attributable net profit rose only 1.34% to NT$12.26bn. Gross margin slipped from 50.54% to 48.02%, restricting earnings conversion and making the growth valuation harder to sustain. Third-quarter performance slightly missed published expectations.

The September-quarter EPS of NT$8.05 was below a vendor estimate of NT$8.14, and reported revenue NT$37.73bn versus NT$38.27bn estimated. These comparisons predate the November selloff and do not demonstrate direct causation. Google Finance Q3 calendar · 29 Oct REPORTED CONSENSUS COMPARISON

October demand offers a genuine positive counterweight.

The issuer's October 2026 monthly net sales increased 32.11% year-on-year to NT$11.973bn, even though they fell 3.26% month-on-month. This supports demand resilience but cannot by itself prove stronger margins or cash flow. Issuer monthly revenue · 6 Nov CONFIRMED FACT

E. Growth opportunities

Ethernet and higher-speed networks: controllers, PHY and switching chips benefit if multi-gigabit enterprise, home and infrastructure refreshes convert into profitable unit shipments. Wi-Fi / Bluetooth and IoT: higher-bandwidth connectivity and connected-device designs could lift chip content, but mix and competition matter. Automotive and digital multimedia: automotive Ethernet, audio and video processing diversify uses from the PC market, subject to design-win timing and pricing.

These are portfolio-specific opportunities identified from the company business description; no unannounced customer order, forecast sales contribution or proven 2027 margin uplift is assumed.
