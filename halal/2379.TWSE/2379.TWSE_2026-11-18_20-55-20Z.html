Why the price moved

A. Why did Realtek rebound while its profit margin remains under pressure?

The bounce was stronger than the broad market, but solid revenue growth has yet to flow proportionally into nine-month bottom-line earnings.

The stock rose from NT$717 on 17 Nov to NT$734 on 18 Nov (+2.37%), while the official TAIEX rose from 40,092.11 to 40,194.91 (+0.26%). That +2.11-point gap is an observed relative move, not proof of a specific institution, trade or announcement.

Issuer-reported October consolidated sales were NT$11.973bn, up 32.11% year on year, and sales for January–October grew 17.24%. This is credible evidence of demand, but the October figure was public before 18 Nov and does not establish why buyers stepped in on that specific day.

Higher sales are not translating into comparable profits

Realtek Q3 revenue increased 27.9% year on year while quarterly net income rose around 20.3%; for nine months, revenue gained roughly 15.85% but profit grew only around 1.34%. Nine-month gross margin decreased from 50.54% to 48.02%, explaining why investors may require stronger proof of profit conversion.
