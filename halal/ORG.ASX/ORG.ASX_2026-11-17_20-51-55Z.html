Why the price moved

A. Why did ORG rise while the Australian market fell?

The 17 November rally happened during a broad selloff, but verified sector resilience and preceding business news do not establish why individual traders bought the shares.

↗
Origin outperformed during a utilities-sector rally.

On 17 November ORG rose from A$12.36 to A$12.56 (+1.62%), while the ASX 200 lost 1.79% to 8,883.20. The utilities sector rose about 1.52%; sector rotation is a plausible contributor, but no same-day issuer announcement proves a direct cause. Market coverage · 17 Nov · CONFIRMED FACT / price causation unverified

♢
Higher LNG selling prices improved quarterly revenue.

Origin reported APLNG September-quarter revenue of A$2.601bn, up 32% quarter on quarter, largely due to realised LNG prices. This may support distributions to Origin. It is contextual operating evidence from 30 October, not proof of the 17 November price move. Origin quarter · 30 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT / plausible price impact

▤
Cash generation improved while underlying profit fell.

FY26 adjusted free cash flow rose to A$2.074bn from A$1.207bn, but underlying profit fell to A$1.159bn from A$1.490bn. Lower tax paid contributed to cash improvement, and weaker gas earnings and depreciation constrained profit. Durable earnings quality remains a question. Origin FY26 · 13 Aug · CONFIRMED FACT / causal market inference unverified

⚡
Battery rollout and electricity volumes support optionality.

Origin reported electricity sales volumes up 3% year-on-year in the September quarter and Eraring Battery 2 on track for the March quarter of 2027. The assets could improve earnings resilience but returns depend on spreads, uptime and capital discipline. Their relevance to the latest daily share move is unproven. Origin quarter · 30 Oct · CONFIRMED FACT / plausible contributor
