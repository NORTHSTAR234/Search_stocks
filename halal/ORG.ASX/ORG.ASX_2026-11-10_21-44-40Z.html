E. Growth opportunities

1. Electricity demand and batteries. September-quarter electricity sales volumes grew 3% year-on-year, partly because of data-centre demand. Mortlake Battery’s 300 MW / 650 MWh achieved commercial operation; Eraring Battery 2 is scheduled for March quarter 2027, expanding dispatch flexibility and potentially smoothing earnings.

2. LNG price leverage and domestic gas. APLNG September-quarter revenue increased 32% sequentially to A$2.601bn (100% APLNG basis) on higher LNG prices. The opportunity is a cash-flow tailwind if commodity markets remain supportive, against production-decline risk.

3. Octopus and Kraken technology. Octopus reached 19m customer accounts in FY26; Kraken reached 95m contracted accounts, and FY27 revenue growth management guidance exceeds 20%. Continued contract conversion could add software-like earnings, but spend and valuation uncertainty remain high.

The Origin-backed Golden Beach gas project's reported early-November administration adds project-specific uncertainty; exposure and direct financial effect were not quantified here.

Watch triggers: clear guidance downgrade, repeated poor adjusted cash conversion, dividend cut, unexpected capital requirements, or a price break below support with weak operating fundamentals. Recheck Musaffa classification before placing an order.
