Investment thesis and why the price moved

Origin owns integrated Australian energy retail and generation operations, Australia Pacific LNG exposure and an economic stake in Octopus Energy and Kraken. The portfolio is diverse, but gas-price and high capital-intensity exposures create uneven earnings.
