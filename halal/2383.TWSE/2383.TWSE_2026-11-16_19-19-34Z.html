Why the price moved

A. Why did the shares plunge despite strong sales and profit?

Elite's operations look stronger than its stock chart, because investors are repricing exposure to AI demand while paying a still-rich multiple for future growth.

↘
A broad Taiwan technology sell-off intensified losses.

On 16 November, TAIEX fell 6.03% while Elite Material lost 9.96%. Taiwan's news agency reported selling in AI-linked electronics and Nvidia-related uncertainty; this helps explain the context, but does not establish how much of Elite's drop any single headline caused.
