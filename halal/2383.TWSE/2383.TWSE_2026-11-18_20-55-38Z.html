E. Growth opportunities and constraints

  • AI server and networking upgrades: higher-speed copper-clad laminates and prepregs may be advantaged as transmission specifications rise; product volume and pricing still need monitoring.
  • Higher-grade material mix: sell-side research reported M7+ mix near 65–70% in Q3 versus 60–65% Q2. These are broker estimates, not newly issuer-certified customer contracts.
  • Capacity and order visibility: additions in Taiwan/China/Penang could support future sales, but reported timing delays, customer concentration, working-capital and equipment spending can dilute near-term free cash flow.
  • Competitive threats: alternative substrate technologies, high-end glass fabric availability, pricing pressure, a change in AI capital spending and export restrictions could reduce the expected multiple.

Recent strong issuer results support growth but do not prove that future EPS, margins or valuation will follow the most optimistic assumptions. Issuer reporting · Revenue filings.
