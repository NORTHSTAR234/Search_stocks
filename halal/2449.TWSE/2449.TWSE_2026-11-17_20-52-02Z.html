A · rating and explanations
Why the price moved

A. Why did KYEC fall even while sales grew?

The company's latest strong sales figures coexist with lower tester utilisation and weaker cash conversion, while Taiwan's semiconductor-heavy market has been repricing rapidly.

Aligned market check, 10–17 November 2026 completed closes: KYEC NT$227.50 → NT$176.50 (-22.4%); official TAIEX 42,711.92 → 40,092.11 (-6.1%); stock underperformance -16.28 percentage points. These are contemporaneous price observations, not proof of a specific cause. KYEC history · official TAIEX index

▤
Testing capacity is earning less despite sales growth.

The issuer reports Q3 revenue of NT$12.142bn, about 30.7% above Q3 2025, yet gross margin fell to 37.6% from 39.5% in Q2, while tester utilisation declined from 68.4% to 65.4%. This lowers the return on the company's expensive equipment, a plausible valuation concern rather than proof of the trading-day cause. KYEC Q3 presentation · Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED OPERATING FACT · PRICE LINK PLAUSIBLE

◷
Capital spending is outpacing operating cash generation.

The November issuer deck shows Q3 operating cash flow of NT$6.063bn versus capex of NT$9.285bn, implying simple free cash flow of approximately −NT$3.222bn. September financial debt was NT$43.032bn against cash of NT$17.744bn, so the investment ramp carries financing and payback risk even if demand stays healthy. KYEC cash-flow and debt slides · Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED OPERATING FACT · PRICE LINK PLAUSIBLE

⌁
Market-wide technology selling intensified the share-price decline.

The official TAIEX lost 6.13% from the 10 November close to 17 November, while KYEC fell 22.42% across the same completed-session window. The index decline confirms broad pressure, but KYEC's much larger loss cannot be attributed to this factor alone without direct evidence of trading motives. Analysts have reduced their near-term earnings expectations.

A 13 November FactSet survey reported a 2026 median EPS estimate cut from NT$8.12 to NT$8.06, following a 6 November cut from NT$8.38 to NT$8.12. The revisions are modest relative to the share-price drop, and their timing does not prove they caused the subsequent selloff. E · business opportunities

E. Growth opportunities

AI GPU, CPU and ASIC testing: complex chips may need more wafer probing, burn-in, final test and capacity time. Rising order volume could improve utilisation, but the company must actually qualify and book customer workloads. KYEC operations deck

Improvement in existing-capacity use: the issuer describes more than 6,800 installed test machines and Q3 utilisation of 65.4%. Recovering towards Q2's 68.4% may improve margins if demand, mix and operating costs cooperate; capacity growth alone does not ensure earnings. Issuer deck

Expansion and geographic diversification: the group plans additional test capacity, including a US investment project reported in July 2026 of up to US$1.4bn. It may broaden customers and resilience, but carries facility, depreciation and financing execution risks; approvals are not committed revenue. Reuters · 10 Jul 2026
