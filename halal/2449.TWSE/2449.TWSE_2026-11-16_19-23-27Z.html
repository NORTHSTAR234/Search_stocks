Revenue growth does not fully translate into incremental profit or available cash. These are dated observations and possible explanations, not proof of who sold on Monday.

E. Growth opportunities

AI GPU/ASIC/CPU testing: demand for complex wafer probing, burn-in and final testing may increase the company's mix and volumes; test utilisation must improve for operating leverage to follow. KYEC capabilities.

Large installed base: the company's deck describes more than 6,800 test systems, so profitable use of existing capacity can boost cash returns without proportionate expansion. Underuse is the opposite risk. KYEC deck.

New capacity/overseas footprint: could broaden customer access and resilience, but construction, depreciation, qualification and financing can lower near-term shareholder returns. A July report mentioned up to US$1.4bn US investment authorisation; this is not committed revenue. CNA July 2026.
