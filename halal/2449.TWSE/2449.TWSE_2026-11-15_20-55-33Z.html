E. Growth and competitive positioning

KYEC has a defensible outsourced wafer-probe, final-test, burn-in and system-test business; the issuer describes more than 6,800 testing systems and says roughly 98% of revenue comes from test services. Q3 2026 exposure by end application was about 37.3% data processing and 29.4% consumer—AI/HPC is an opportunity, but not every KYEC sale can be presumed Nvidia or AI revenue. The company reports 48% of the global top-50 semiconductor firms use KYEC testing services (issuer claim). KYEC issuer slide deck.

US testing plant authorisation (maximum US$1.4bn), the Singapore expansion and new equipment can raise geographical reach and future utilisation if customer volumes materialise. The opposing scenario is investment outrunning committed demand, creating low utilisation, debt costs, depreciation and diluted returns on capital. Customer-level backlog and confirmed US facility revenue timing were not verifiable: NOT RATED. US investment announcement.
