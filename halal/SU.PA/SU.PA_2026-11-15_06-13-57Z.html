H. Reasons to remain constructive, reasons for caution & independent actions

Reasons to remain constructive

  • Record €11.460bn Q3 with +16.9% organic expansion and raised FY2026 targets.
  • Exposure to hard-to-replace data-centre power, distribution, grid and automation infrastructure; ongoing service and software mix.
  • Category-A ratings were reaffirmed following the PTC announcement, providing some funding reassurance, although ratings do not remove debt risk.

Reasons for caution

  • Large cash PTC deal, share dilution and €6bn bond issue complicate per-share valuation and post-deal leverage.
  • Price underperforming CAC 40 since 19 October; weak 50/200-day moving-average setup and potential €220/228 support retest.
