E. Growth opportunities

Data-centre energy infrastructure: power switching, UPS, cooling and control systems scale with AI equipment demand. Data-centre orders grew strongly in Q3, although future investment cycles may fluctuate.

Grid efficiency and automation: electrification, resilient power supply, industrial automation and energy-saving demand support multi-year revenue and service opportunities.

PTC and industrial AI software: design/production lifecycle software could improve recurring software mix. Schneider anticipates €250m of annual run-rate cost synergies by Year 3 and about €800m in revenue synergies; these are contingent forecasts, not realised cash flows. Acquisition plan.
