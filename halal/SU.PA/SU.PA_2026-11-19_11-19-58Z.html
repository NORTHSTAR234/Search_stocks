A · Business and movement analysis

Why the price moved

A. Why has Schneider Electric struggled despite strong sales growth?

Sales and margins can improve even while investors reduce the valuation they assign to future per-share cash flows after a costly acquisition, new shares and additional interest costs. None of the deal items independently proves the cause of the one-day rebound.

◇
The PTC acquisition has changed the market's valuation assumptions.

The 5 October 2026 agreement values PTC equity at about US$22.6bn (€20.1bn). Software and industrial integration can improve recurring revenue, but the proposed purchase raises the hurdle for return on invested capital. It is a plausible multi-week valuation contributor, not a proven explanation of the 18 November share rise. PTC/Schneider · 5 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED DEAL / PLAUSIBLE PRICE CONTRIBUTOR

▣
Equity issuance means earnings are shared across more shares.

Schneider announced a €6.0bn capital placement of 25m new shares priced at €240 on 20 October. This raises deal cash but dilutes existing share ownership and potential per-share growth. The €240 issue price is a historical transaction term, not guaranteed technical support. Issuer · 20 Oct 2026 · CONFIRMED FINANCING

≋
A fresh US-dollar bond issue changes interest costs.

A 17 November pricing, disclosed on 18 November, involved US$10bn of senior bonds with around 5.7% average coupons and 10.2-year average maturity, in addition to earlier euro funding. Cash interest and leverage can affect future free cash flow. Completion economics, foreign-exchange hedges and post-deal net debt still need monitoring. Issuer · 18 Nov 2026 · CONFIRMED FINANCING / PRICE CAUSE UNPROVEN

↗
Demand for power equipment remains unusually strong.

Schneider reported €11.46bn Q3 2026 revenue and +16.9% organic growth, upgrading its 2026 organic adjusted EBITA growth objective to +18–21%. These are genuine operating positives that offset some deal concerns. A Q3 revenue statement cannot establish future post-acquisition net income or price direction. Growth opportunities: global electrification, grid modernisation and AI data-centre power equipment and cooling; process and factory automation, plus higher-value digital subscriptions through AVEVA and prospective PTC combination. Schneider's 16 Oct Q3 release reported +16.9% organic revenue growth, led by strong data-centre demand. Continued double-digit growth is plausible but not guaranteed; integration may absorb cash and dilute per-share returns. Issuer Q3 · 16 Oct · PTC transaction · 5 Oct

ValueInvesting.io · 18 Nov reported 23.23× forward P/E for SU.PA versus 22.03× for ABBN.SW on its comparison table; these are analyst-estimate multiples with vendor/period uncertainty, not guaranteed directly comparable consolidated pro-forma earnings. Other platforms have published lower forward multiples with differing assumptions.
