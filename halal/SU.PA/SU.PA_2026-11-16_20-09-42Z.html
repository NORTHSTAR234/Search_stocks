D. Financial resilience and financing

Schneider reported H1 2026 revenue approximately €21.2bn, adjusted EBITA approximately €4.1bn (margin 19.3%) and FCF €1.631bn, versus €0.474bn a year earlier. These figures are pre-PTC financing. PTC equity consideration is US$22.6bn (roughly €20.1bn at transaction-date FX); issuer expected €5–6bn equity and €16–17bn new borrowings, with financing executed in stages. Confirmed October €6bn equity proceeds and €6bn bond issue are not equivalent to €12bn immediately increased net debt; cash proceeds may remain on the balance sheet until used.

Review leverage only with a post-transaction pro forma balance sheet and updated guidance; those verified inputs are absent today. Thus a current post-PTC net-debt / EBITDA or fair-value DCF is NOT VERIFIED.

E. Catalysts and competitive environment

  • Data centres: management reported triple-digit demand growth in Q3, providing power distribution, 3-phase UPS and cooling opportunities, though demand is not the same as revenue.
  • Electrification: grid resilience, energy efficiency and industrial controls are structural demand themes, with material capex sensitivity.
  • PTC software: company estimates €250m cost synergies by year three and approximately €800m revenue synergies, both management goals rather than achieved results.
  • Industrial automation: Q3 +8.1% organic growth broadens the business beyond data centres.
