Why the price moved

Operating growth is strong, but acquisition cash financing, equity dilution and investors' required earnings-per-share returns complicate valuation. The reasons below are credible verified developments, not proven causes of the exact 20 November market order flow.

▣

Industrial software synergies could strengthen services revenue but will require integration and a credible return on the acquisition price. The issuer on 18 November disclosed US$10bn senior bonds priced on 17 November at an average coupon around 5.7%, following a €6bn equity raise and €6bn euro bonds. These are financing principal amounts, not already realised pro-forma net debt, and their effect on per-share cash must be assessed. ⚡
European electricity costs could constrain AI investment.

On 19 November Reuters reported a Schneider executive warning that high electricity costs, inadequate grid investment and fragmented rules may shift European AI data-centre investment elsewhere. This is a market-demand risk, not a company earnings miss or a confirmed share-price catalyst. Reuters · 19 Nov 2026 SOURCE-LINKED; direct daily-price causation not established

↗
Reported underlying business growth is still robust.

The 16 October Q3 release reported €11.46bn revenue, up 16.9% organically, and upgraded 2026 organic adjusted EBITA growth target to 18–21%. This is important positive counterevidence, although revenue growth need not translate into equal EPS or after-deal free cash flow.
