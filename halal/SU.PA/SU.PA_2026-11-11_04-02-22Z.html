E. Growth opportunities

  • Data centres and AI power: triple-digit Q3 data-centre demand growth supports longer backlog and power/cooling/switchgear infrastructure demand.
  • Electrification/automation: energy efficiency, grid modernisation and broad EM adoption support organic growth beyond AI alone; Q3 EM +19% organic.
  • Industrial intelligence software: if PTC closes and integrates well, Schneider projects €250m cost synergies by Year 3 and ~€800m revenue synergies; outcomes are forward-looking, not booked profits.
