E. Catalysts and competitive picture

  • Data centres: Q3 demand increased triple-digit, reflecting hyperscaler power and thermal constraints; potential backlog conversion in 2027–28.
  • Grid and electrification: structurally expanding addressable markets for switchgear, power management, software and services.
  • PTC/Cognite: industrial AI and asset lifecycle potential, contingent on regulatory close and integration, with issuer-targeted €250m cost synergies and €800m revenue synergies.
  • Peers: ABB, Eaton and Legrand offer partial industrial/energy exposure but differ in geography, segment mix and software weighting; no unsupported numeric peer-multiple ranking is used.
