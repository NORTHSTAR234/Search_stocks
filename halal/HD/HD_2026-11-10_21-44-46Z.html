E. Growth opportunities

  • Professional customer expansion: SRS distribution and trade-specialist relationships may raise purchase frequency and wallet share among contractors, improving cross-selling.
  • Maintenance and repair over major remodels: smaller DIY/DIFM projects can sustain demand while households defer financing-sensitive big renovation plans.
  • Digital and fulfillment investment: combined stores, supply chain and online ordering can improve pro delivery reliability and productivity as sales recover.

Upside depends on execution: acquisitions alone do not guarantee organic growth or margins.
