E. Growth opportunities

  • Professional contractors / SRS: enlarged specialty trade and wholesale presence can build repeat higher-ticket B2B purchases and cross-sell; execution and integration costs require monitoring.
  • Digital fulfilment: nationwide express delivery and app-led assistance support convenience, share retention and contractor productivity, but require profitable logistics economics.
  • Housing normalization: lower financing costs, more property transactions and deferred large remodeling projects could lift comps; no timing is guaranteed.
