Why the price moved

A. Why did Home Depot fall after growing sales?

Sales increased, but the latest earnings and fiscal-year guidance showed much less profit growth than revenue growth; investors may be questioning how quickly the business can convert a housing recovery into earnings.

📦
More sales did not produce equally strong profit growth.

On 17 November, Q3 revenue increased 4.9% to $43.4bn and US comparable-store sales rose 2.2%, while adjusted EPS grew only 3.2% to $3.86. Acquired business mix and costs may absorb some revenue growth; the exact share-price contribution is not isolated. Issuer Q3 · 17 Nov

Updated fiscal 2026 guidance calls for about 4.5% sales growth but roughly 2% adjusted EPS growth and around 12.8% adjusted operating margin. Investors may assign a lower valuation when profits lag revenue even though management expects top-line improvement. SEC earnings exhibit · 17 Nov
