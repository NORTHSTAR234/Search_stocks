1. Home Depot investment analysis

A. Why the stock moved — Why did HD rise sharply from October and then hesitate ahead of earnings?

Simple explanation: the multi-week rebound is observable; today's fractional movement cannot confidently be assigned to a single headline. Four factors matter, but temporal association is not proof of trading causation.

🏠
Housing expectations lifted sector sensitivity

HD's October/November rally accompanied changing expectations about future housing and renovation spending. Higher mortgage costs and low housing turnover remain risks to big-ticket projects, even where contractors benefit from maintenance demand.

Industry perspective, market analysis dated 16 Nov 2026; not proof of today's trade causality.

Sales growth is not the same as earnings growth

Q2 sales increased 5.7% year over year, but first-half operating income grew only about 1.1% even while six-month sales grew 5.3%. Selling expenses, acquired-business costs and input costs can absorb revenue gains.
