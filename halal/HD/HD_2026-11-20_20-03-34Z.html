Why the price moved

A. Why did Home Depot shares fall after revenue grew?

Sales improved, but an only modest expected rise in full-year earnings, fewer customer transactions and leadership change leave investors weighing profit quality against the price they pay.

📦
More sales are generating proportionately less earnings growth.

Home Depot reported 17 November Q3 sales of US$43.4bn, up 4.9%; adjusted EPS rose 3.2% to US$3.86, while GAAP EPS was US$3.72. US comparable sales rose 2.2%, but comparable transactions fell 0.5%. Investors may question whether customer demand is durable, though the exact price impact is not isolated. SEC exhibit · 17 Nov CONFIRMED RESULTS · PLAUSIBLE PRICE MECHANISM

📉
Management expects earnings growth to lag sales growth.

Fiscal 2026 outlook is approximately +4.5% sales, +2% adjusted EPS and 12.8% adjusted operating margin, with about +1.5% GAAP EPS growth. A slower earnings trajectory than top-line growth can compress the valuation investors are willing to pay. Guidance is confirmed; investor reaction is an interpretation. Issuer · 17 Nov CONFIRMED GUIDANCE · PLAUSIBLE CONTRIBUTOR

👤
The announced CEO succession adds an execution checkpoint.

On 17 November the board announced longtime executive Richard McPhail would succeed Ted Decker as CEO on 23 November; Decker remains chair. Leadership continuity may reinforce the contractor-distribution strategy, but new roles create questions about capital discipline and margins. The announcement is certain, not its causal effect on the share price. Issuer · 17 Nov CONFIRMED EVENT · PRICE CAUSATION UNVERIFIED
