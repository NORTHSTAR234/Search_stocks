A. Why is Home Depot holding up while housing demand is subdued?

Home Depot has recently outperformed the broad market, but its operating recovery is only partial and expectations matter ahead of quarterly results.

📊
Home Depot held up better than the market.

On 16 Nov 2026, HD fell from $348.69 to $347.51 (−0.34%), while the S&P 500 lost 1.92%. The stock therefore outperformed by 1.58 percentage points on that one session. This is an observed divergence, not proof of a Home Depot-specific catalyst. FRED · 16 Nov 2026 CONFIRMED PRICES; CAUSE UNVERIFIED

Existing-store sales growth is still modest.

The company reported Q2 comparable sales growth of 1.7% (US +1.3%) on 18 Aug 2026, even though total sales grew 5.7%. This means acquisitions and scale can support revenue while underlying customer demand remains subdued. A possible concern is whether profits can keep growing without stronger organic demand.
