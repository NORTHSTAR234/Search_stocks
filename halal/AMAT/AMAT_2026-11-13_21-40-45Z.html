A. Investment thesis and business quality

Applied sells process, inspection and advanced packaging equipment, related software and installed-base services. Process qualification, materials-engineering know-how, and fab-scale service infrastructure support competitive positioning. The AI, foundry/logic, high-bandwidth memory and advanced packaging buildout is the key growth engine. Risks are industry capex cyclicality, China/export policy and high valuation multiples.

Fiscal Q4 revenue rose 53% year over year to $10.41B. Semiconductor Systems demand was particularly strong. Management expects wafer-fab equipment spending to grow more than 30% in 2027, subject to supply-chain capacity and customer ramp execution. This is a company outlook, not a guaranteed industry outcome.
