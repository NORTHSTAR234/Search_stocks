A. Investment thesis & score

Applied Materials supplies deposition, etch, inspection, advanced packaging, process control and recurring service solutions for logic, memory and AI accelerators. Customer qualification creates switching friction. Investment score 78/100 recognises technology position and cash generation, discounted for semicap cyclicality, geopolitical exposure, crowded AI capex expectations and chart deterioration. Entry score 25/100 reflects weak risk/reward at current price and old no-chase threshold.
