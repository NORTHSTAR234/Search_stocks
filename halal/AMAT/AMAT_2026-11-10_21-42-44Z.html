E. Growth opportunities

  • Advanced logic/gate-all-around: transistor and interconnect materials complexity boosts process-step intensity and integration content.
  • DRAM/HBM and advanced packaging: chip stacking, hybrid bonding and new memory architectures demand deposition, polishing and inspection tools. Applied announced collaboration initiatives with Intel and Kioxia in October/September 2026.
  • Recurring services and EPIC partnerships: installed-base spare parts and service can buffer equipment-cycle volatility. The company's $5B EPIC Center research investment increases joint development with chipmakers, but entails investment and execution risk.
