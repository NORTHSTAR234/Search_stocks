A. Why is AMAT falling despite record earnings and growing sales?

AMAT posted rapidly growing sales and profit, but valuation, sector volatility and post-earnings positioning can still pressure the share price. No single cause of the sell-off has been proven.

Record profits have not protected the stock.

Applied reported Q4 revenue of US$10.41B, up 53%, and non-GAAP EPS of US$4.16, up 92%, on 12 November. AMAT then closed 16 November at US$350.25 after US$370.50 on 13 November (−5.47%, consolidated), illustrating that good results can coexist with profit-taking; this is a possible interpretation, not proven motivation. AI demand creates growth, but high expectations matter.

Management guided fiscal Q1 2027 revenue to about US$11.1B ±US$0.5B and adjusted EPS to US$4.35 ±US$0.20. Those figures and a forecast for WFE demand growth above 30% are bullish operating signals, yet at a 27.2× trailing FY2026 adjusted EPS multiple, a negative valuation reset remains possible.
