E. Growth opportunities

  • Advanced smartphone displays and AMOLED drivers: address higher-specification phone and mobile panel demand; Q3 seasonal orders are already visible, but the timing of lasting gains is unverified.
  • Vehicle screens and driver/touch integration: increasing screen area may expand addressable semiconductor content over time, subject to design wins and auto qualifications.
  • Imaging, MEMC and edge-AI SoCs: broadens product mix beyond commodity display drivers; commercially meaningful 2027 incremental revenue has not been quantified in verified filings.

F. Risks and upcoming events

Core risks: consumer display demand and price competition; wafer, memory, packaging and test cost inflation; working-capital inventory build; concentrated end-market cycles; Taiwan supply-chain and cross-strait geopolitical exposure. The Q4 margin guide's downward shift makes pricing and mix central to the thesis.
