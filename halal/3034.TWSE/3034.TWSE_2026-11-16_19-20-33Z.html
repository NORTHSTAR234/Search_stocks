A · Investment thesis and market drivers
Why the price moved

A. Why has Novatek fallen despite stronger Q3 earnings?

The latest quarter grew strongly, but investors face weak Q4 management guidance and unusually heavy Taiwan equity volatility. These observations help frame the risk; none establishes the complete causal explanation of the selloff.

◉
The next quarter looks weaker than the last one.

At its 6 Nov earnings call, Novatek guided Q4 revenue to NT$28–29.2bn and gross margin to 37–39%, versus Q3 sales NT$30.971bn and margin 40.89%. Management pointed to year-end panel inventory controls and cost pressure. This is a confirmed forward-looking caution, but the share-price decline began before the guidance.
