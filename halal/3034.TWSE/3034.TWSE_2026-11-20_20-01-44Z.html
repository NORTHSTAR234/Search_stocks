E · Long-term thesis & competition

Display and visual-SoC franchises

Novatek designs driver ICs used in phones, OLED/AMOLED and other displays, plus timing controllers, visual processors and related SoCs. Premium phone and automotive display integration may raise content per device; large TV/monitor panel exposure, competitive pricing, semiconductor cycle volatility and concentrated Asian electronics customers are material risks. Reported design wins and new-feature stories are NOT assigned invented future sales or margins. Monitor customer order cycles, inventory conversion, Q4 guided margin and product mix.
