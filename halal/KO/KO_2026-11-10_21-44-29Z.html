Volume grew 5% in Q3. FY2026 guidance raised to at least 11% comparable EPS growth and at least $13B free cash flow, with first nine months free cash flow $11.4B (company non-GAAP measures). Analyst FY2027 adjusted EPS consensus is ~$3.53 versus ~$3.34 in FY2026, implying ~5.7% growth. At $96.91, KO trades at 29.0× 2026E and 27.5× 2027E EPS: expensive for its expected growth, even with a brand moat.

Strongest factors: scale and global distribution; resilient cash generation and pricing power. Weakest factors: premium multiple and low margin of safety; commodity/logistics and currency sensitivity, plus debt/tax litigation exposure. Comparisons with beverage peers should account for their different franchises and bottling mixes.

E. Growth opportunities

  1. Global beverage volume: 5% Q3 unit-case growth and share gains expand the base on which concentrate and distribution economics operate.
  2. Portfolio mix and premium products: water, sports drinks, coffee, value-added dairy and zero-sugar beverages diversify beyond core colas, with scope for higher-revenue mix.
  3. System optimisation: African bottling divestiture could improve the asset-light earnings mix and capital allocation, but closing requires regulatory approval and produces headline revenue headwinds.
