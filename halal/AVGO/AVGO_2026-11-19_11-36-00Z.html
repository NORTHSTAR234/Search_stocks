A. Why has Broadcom bounced despite falling far from its peak?

Why the price moved

The company has delivered rapid AI growth, yet its share price remains materially below the June high; operating momentum and investor valuation expectations are different things.

◈
AI chip sales provide a strong earnings foundation.

Broadcom reported fiscal Q3 AI semiconductor revenue of US$16.7 billion, up 221% year-on-year, while companywide revenue reached US$29.6 billion, up 86%. For the fiscal quarter ended 2 August, Broadcom reported US$14.2 billion operating cash inflow and US$0.5 billion capital expenditure, leaving US$13.7 billion free cash flow. That supports capacity to service obligations and pay dividends; it does not rule out future AI investment needs or weaker customer orders.
