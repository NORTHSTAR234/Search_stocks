E. Growth opportunity and investment thesis

Fortune manufactures transformers, distribution and switching equipment that can benefit from grid replacement, high-voltage connections, renewable integration and power-intensive industrial growth. Management has discussed a future Taichung production facility expected around Q2 2027, but the schedule and customer order conversion require subsequent official confirmation, not assumed 2026 earnings.

Investment thesis: improving gross margin and cash collections would validate the long-duration grid opportunity, while back-to-back weak monthly sales or capital expansion failing to convert into revenue would justify a lower multiple. The main bearish mechanism is not a disappearing transformer market; it is a high expectation multiple paired with uneven timing, financial review limitations and significant market volatility.
